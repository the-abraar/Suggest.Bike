# Traffic signs, signals, markings (ট্রাফিক সাইন ও সংকেত)

Access date: 2026-10-13.

## What the law says
- Act s.2(14): "traffic sign or signal" (ট্রাফিক সাইন বা সংকেত) means every sign, signal, marking, device or light conforming to the "National Traffic Sign Manual" (জাতীয় ট্রাফিক সাইন ম্যানুয়াল) or as government approves, shown by a public body or its officer to inform, guide, warn or control traffic [A].
- s.42(1): government or authorised body may install, replace or remove signs; **42(2): every driver, pedestrian and road user must obey signs and signals**; 42(3): use zebra crossing/footbridge/underpass where available; 42(4): uniformed police or vehicle inspector may order a driver to stop. Violation: s.85, up to 1 month / Tk 10,000 / 1 point [A].
- The Road Transport Rules 2022 reproduce a sign chart. Pages around 19632-19640 of the gazette show colour pictograms: "mandatory signs" (বাধ্যতামূলক চিহ্ন), road markings (রোড মার্কিং) and signal-related items - lane lines (continuous, broken, edge), no-entry-without-necessity hatch markings, pedestrian crossings, no-parking yellow lines, box junctions, lane-direction arrows. I viewed contact-sheet images only; I did not transcribe every sign's official Bangla caption, so individual sign meanings are NOT verified here. Use the gazette PDF pages 19632-19640 [B].

## Signals (general knowledge, flagged)
- Red = stop, amber = prepare, green = go is the standard convention under the Vienna Convention family; Bangladesh sign manual follows it (I did not find a statute sentence stating it). Pedestrian signal figure shown in the gazette page 19632 images (red standing man / green walking man), caption not read.
- Traffic police hand signals override lights (general practice; no text verified).

## Penalty
Signal violation (red light jumping) = s.42 offence, s.85 penalty; DMP AI cameras now issue notices by letter/SMS (see dhaka-and-city-rules.md).
