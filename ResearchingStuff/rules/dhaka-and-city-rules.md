# Dhaka and city-specific rules (ঢাকা ও মহানগর)

Access date: 2026-10-13.

## 1. Who enforces
- DMP (ঢাকা মেট্রোপলিটন পুলিশ) Traffic Division; in metro areas the DC (Traffic) is the authority for unpaid on-spot fines (Act s.108(3)) [A]. BRTA notifies areas where officers can fine on the spot (s.113) [A]. DTCA (ঢাকা পরিবহন সমন্বয় কর্তৃপক্ষ) coordinates transport in Dhaka, Gazipur, Narayanganj, Munshiganj, Narsingdi and Manikganj [B].

## 2. Speed
- Motorcycles 30 km/h inside city corporations, municipalities and district towns (BRTA Speed Limit Guideline 2024; announced 8 May 2024) [C]. Enforcement gradual, speed guns, mobile courts.

## 3. Fines, e-ticketing, AI cameras (e-challan equivalent)
- DMP began issuing auto-generated notices from CCTV/AI-camera footage for violations on various Dhaka roads; if the owner fails to appear/pay after the notice, summons or arrest warrants follow (Dhaka Tribune, with bdnews24 May 2026) [D][E]. The DMP traffic division began a trial of AI cameras on 7 May 2026 [F]; digital cases are filed against the vehicle owner.
- How it reaches you: a letter signed by the traffic division/Traffic Technical Unit at the registered address; SMS only from 01320-042207 and 01320-042227; payment through approved online banking platforms "including Upay and CBL" (DMP warning 25 May 2026) [D]. DMP will never ask for PIN/OTP. Fake SMS fine scams are circulating [D].
- A bdnews24 report (May 2026) says notice recipients must pay at DMP HQ or the traffic division through banks or mobile financial services [E].
- Chattogram police introduced e-fine payment earlier (Dhaka Tribune 378567, snippet) [G]. Highway Police auto-fine on Dhaka-Chattogram highway (bKash/Nagad) is not a DMP system [H].
- Older systems (e-Traffic Prosecution, UCash, Robi SMS payment) may be defunct; not confirmed [G].
- Physical enforcement: see penalties-table.md section D (charge form, on-spot fine, vehicle to police station, dumping yard, mobile court).
- Plates: DMP announced action vs missing BRTA digital plates/RFID; AI cameras need front plates for bikes - front plate decision pending [I][J].

## 4. Parking and lanes
- Parking only in designated areas (Act s.47; fine up to Tk 5,000 + 1 point) [K]. City corporations/DTCA designate parking; parking operators charge a fee approved by the transport committee (s.47(4)).
- Dedicated motorcycle lane: no legal text found in this research (not asserted).
- Footpath riding illegal (s.49(1)(ঠ)).

## 5. Ride-sharing (রাইড শেয়ারিং) - Pathao, Uber, etc.
- Legal base: "Ride-Sharing Service Guidelines 2017" (রাইড শেয়ারিং সেবা নির্দেশিকা ২০১৭) requires ride-sharing companies and the owners of the vehicles to hold BRTA enlistment certificates; BRTA approved 12 companies including Pathao and Uber (UNB) [L]. Observer (2 Aug 2026): nearly eight years later key provisions remain partly enforced; complaints about cancellations, extra fares, surge pricing [M].
- Act link: a motorcycle hired for a fare is a "contract carriage" (s.2(8)); s.124(1)(ট) lets government order policy on ride sharing; s.31 and s.79 penalise commercial use without permission [K].
- Motorcycle ride sharing was initially excluded (Financial Express, June 2020: "Govt allows ride sharing services except motorbikes") [N] and then allowed under a BRTA circular dated 3 Sep 2020: only motorcycles holding a BRTA enlistment certificate (1,156 at the time, out of about 1.04 lakh linked to ride-hailing firms), inside the DTCA area, rider and passenger both in standard helmets, disinfecting rules [B]. Those conditions were COVID-era; whether they were relaxed later: unconfirmed.
- A Daily Star editorial and DT report on a draft BRTA "Motorcycle Operation Control Guideline" exist; status unconfirmed [L2].

## 6. Pillion and special groups
- Law allows only one pillion; no gender-specific rule found. Draft 2023 policy would have barred pregnant women, elders, and children under 12 from motorbike passenger seats (draft only) [O].

## 7. Common police scenarios and rider rights (all derived from Act text; not legal advice)
1. Police may stop you and ask for papers only if SI/sergeant or above, or an authorised BRTA inspector (ss.109, 113); a constable may signal you to stop (s.42(4) says "uniformed police officer" may order a stop).
2. For fineable offences you should receive a written charge form and a receipt; you sign or thumbprint (s.108(1), 113(2)). Refusing the copy can lead to warrantless arrest (s.108(4)).
3. Licence can be held only with a temporary receipt allowing you to keep riding until its date (s.111(3)).
4. Vehicle impound: apply within 2 months for release (s.115(3)); the owner must be told within 24 hours where the vehicle/driver was taken after arrest (s.110(3)).
5. Extortion: demanding unauthorised money/terminal charges is "extortion" under the Penal Code (s.83 relates to terminal tolls) [K].
6. Paper originals: the Act lists registration, fitness, insurance, permit as papers that can be inspected; digital copies' acceptance is not covered in the Act text (BRTA's online/digital certificate practice not verified).
