# Helmet and rider safety rules (হেলমেট ও নিরাপত্তা)

Access date: 2026-10-13.

## 1. The legal rule
- **Act s.49(1) part 1 (চ)**: besides the driver, a motorcycle may carry no more than one pillion (সহযাত্রী), and both driver and pillion must wear helmets properly (যথাযথভাবে) [A].
- Penalty: s.92(1) - up to 3 months jail, or up to Tk 10,000, or both, plus 1 demerit point to the driver; bailable, compoundable (s.117(1)(গ)), warrantless arrest possible under s.110(1) for s.92(1) offences; on-the-spot fine and mobile court possible (ss.113, 114(2)) [A].
- The Act does not name a helmet standard, does not exempt women, children or passengers on any basis, and does not fix a minimum age for a pillion. Chin strap and "properly" (যথাযথভাবে) are the only quality words. [A]
- Triple riding is the same offence (same clause, same penalty). [A]
- Mobile phone use while riding: s.49(1) part 2 (ক), penalty s.92(2) up to 1 month / Tk 5,000 / 1 point [A].

## 2. Helmet standard
- BSTI standard **BDS 1136:2022** covers helmets for motorcycle, scooter and electric two-wheeler riders; it revises a 1986 standard and addresses shell, impact absorption, retention strap and visor type; BikeBD says there is not enough public information to judge parity with ECE 22.06 and does not say it is mandatory [B].
- As of 1 Mar 2025 (Financial Express) BRTA had BSTI guidelines but "no directive has been issued yet to enforce their use"; an expert committee recommended barcodes/QR codes on helmets so police can check conformity; BRTA chairman said guidelines would follow "within next week" [C]. I found no confirmed gazette making BDS 1136 mandatory (status unresolved).
- ARI survey cited in [C]: only 10% of riders and 2% of pillions use high-quality helmets.
- BSTI director general (Feb 2025, per BRAC/Prothom Alo summary): standard helmets cost roughly Tk 3,000 to Tk 70,000 and buyers should pick BSTI-certified ones [D].
- Gulshan traffic division (DMP) ran a "break the cheap helmet" programme and urged BRTA-approved helmets, saying riders buy cheap low-quality helmets to avoid fines [E].

## 3. Enforcement drives
- "No helmet, no fuel" (নো হেলমেট, নো ফুয়েল): police field units were directed on 16 May 2024 by Additional IGP (Crime & Operations) Md. Atiqul Islam at the monthly crime meeting, following a directive of Minister Obaidul Quader; applies nationwide, riders and pillions; no fine figure in the article [F]. A Prothom Alo piece quotes a police official that the policy made helmet use universal among Dhaka riders [D2] (secondary mention).
- Earlier: deputy commissioners were told to ensure helmets (Daily Star Bangla news-443991, snippet only) [G]; Dinajpur one-drive example "225 cases, Tk 7 lakh fined in two hours" (2022) is general traffic enforcement, not helmet-specific [H].
- Practical on-spot fines: the Act sets maximum only; actual amounts come from the officer under s.108(2)/s.113. No official per-violation spot-fine schedule found.

## 4. Pillion and passenger rules
- Maximum one pillion (s.49(1)(চ)).
- Draft only: the 2023 "Motorcycle Movement Policy" proposed barring pillions on highways and barring pregnant women, elderly and children under 12 from motorcycle passenger seats; set a 30 km/h city limit; requires licence at sale. Status: draft, not confirmed enacted [I].
- Safety statistics (ARI/PWA, via FE 1 Mar 2025): 2024 - 9,085 men and 714 women motorcycle accident victims, 2,362 deaths; 92.33% of female victims were pillion passengers; 78.62% of victims were aged 15-23; motorcycles caused 43.42% of road deaths in Jan 2025; standard helmets cut severe head injury likelihood by 69% [C]. BRTA reported 1,706 motorcycle deaths in 2024 (BRAC summary) - the two figures differ by source/definition [D].

## 5. Night riding and lights
- No specific night-riding ban in the Act. s.49(1) part 2 (জ): no high beam that disturbs oncoming drivers at night (penalty s.92(2)) [A].
- s.40(3): lights, reflectors, signal lights and horn must match BRTA technical specifications; unapproved changes up to s.84 (1-3 years) [A]. Headlight-on-in-daytime rule: not found in sources (not asserted).
- Horn/noise: s.45; silent zones; penalty s.88. Noise level numbers are set by gazette under Environment Conservation law (s.45(6)); not extracted.

## 6. Other
- Speed limits that apply to bikes: see roads-and-restrictions.md.
- Women riders: no gender-specific restriction in the Act; s.124(1)(জ) only encourages women drivers [A].
