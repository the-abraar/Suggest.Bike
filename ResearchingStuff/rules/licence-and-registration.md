# Licence, registration, fees, plates (ড্রাইভিং লাইসেন্স ও রেজিস্ট্রেশন)

Access date: 2026-10-13.

## 1. Driving licence (ড্রাইভিং লাইসেন্স)
- Legal conditions (Act s.6(2)): age at least 18 for a non-professional (অপেশাদার) licence, 21 for professional (পেশাদার); at least class 8 pass; physically and mentally fit; pass the driving competence test; other conditions by rules. Application with NID copy and fee (s.6(1)). Licence valid nationwide, not transferable (s.6(4),(5)). [A]
- Learner licence (শিক্ষানবিশ ড্রাইভিং লাইসেন্স): required to practise lawfully (s.4(1)); not given the 12 points (s.11(1)). [A]
- Process (secondary sources, not official BRTA page - verify at BRTA): learner licence first, then permanent smart card; the exam covers biometric capture, written, oral and practical tests in one visit; smart card is posted; documents - passport photo, medical certificate from a registered doctor, NID, education certificate, learner pass proof; photo file max 150 KB, others 600 KB [F][G]. Online route "BSP" (bsp.brta.gov.bd) named by sources, current status unconfirmed.
- Rules Schedule 2 lists the test content: Part (ক) traffic rules, signals, signs and markings, lighting at night, parking, pedestrian crossing, lane discipline, emergencies; practical items include starting engine, moving off, gear shifting, overtaking, turning left/right, stopping/parking, reversing, hill start (page 19620 of gazette) [B]. Schedule 2(ক) "Rules for driving" and 2(খ) hand signals are on pages 19621-19622 [B].
- Test applicant must also certify knowledge of Rules 129-140 (rules text, page 19620) [B].
- **Categories**: Act s.4(3) leaves categories to the Rules. I did not extract the category table (Rules ch. on licensing) from the 191-page gazette; Rules Schedule 1 item 8 shows a fee for "adding a vehicle class to a licence" (Tk 400), so multi-class licences exist [B]. Motorcycle class name/cc split: NOT VERIFIED.

### Points system (পয়েন্ট সিস্টেম)
- 12 points per licence (Act s.11(1)). Deductions from Schedule 4 of the Rules (referenced by Rule 20(2),(3)); table read from gazette page 19642 [B]:
  - s.42 signs/signals: 1; s.31 commercial use: 1; s.34 fare chart: 1; s.35 contract-carriage meter: 1; s.43 overload: 2; s.44 speed: 1; s.45 noise/horn: 1; s.46 pollution/unfit: 1; s.47 parking: 1; s.48 highway use: 1; s.49 general instructions: 1; s.62 accident aid: 1; wilful obstruction: 2.
- Zero points = suspension/cancellation risk. BRTA announced the Road Safety Penalty System (RSPS) app on 5 May 2026 "to launch simultaneously across the country"; no launch date, no payment details [H].
- Review of points: application fee Tk 200 (Schedule 1 item 11) [B].

## 2. Registration (রেজিস্ট্রেশন)
- Must register where owner's address falls (s.19) and display number plate (s.16(3)). Transfer: 30 days (seller) / 60 days (buyer) (s.21). [A]
- **Motorcycle registration fee (Rules Schedule 1, item 21)**: engine up to 100 cc (or electric motor up to 5 kW): Tk 2,000; above 100 cc (or electric above 5 kW): Tk 3,000 [B]. (Prothom Alo reported earlier that the fee for up to 100 cc would fall from Tk 4,200 to Tk 2,000 - consistent with Rules 2022; article date not captured [I].)
- Three-wheelers (auto-rickshaw, tempo): up to 100 cc Tk 1,000; above Tk 1,800 (item 22) [B]. Ownership change fee for three-wheelers: same as the registration fee; address change fee Tk 500 (item 20) [B]. Disabled-person vehicle Tk 25 (item 23) [B]. Temporary registration: half of the regular fee (item 15) [B]. Duplicate registration certificate Tk 500; hire-purchase entry Tk 2,400; modification fee Tk 1,000 (items 16, 18, 19) [B].
- Other Schedule 1 fees possibly relevant: vehicle inspection Tk 800; fitness certificate duplicate Tk 300; annual fitness fee Tk 800 for non-heavy vehicles and Tk 1,500 for heavy (items 30-33) - applicability to motorcycles NOT VERIFIED (Act s.25 applies to every motor vehicle; in practice motorcycles renew fitness with the tax token - confirm with BRTA) [B].
- The Financial Assistance Fund contribution is paid with registration (s.16(2)); amount per the Rules - not extracted.
- Electric two-wheelers are covered by the 5 kW split above.

## 3. CC limit (সিসি সীমা)
- The Act has no cc cap. A notification of the Roads Transport and Highways Division/BRTA (via Ministry of Home Affairs meeting decision), reported 11 Oct 2023, allows registration and riding of **locally manufactured bikes up to 375 cc**; previously the road limit was 165 cc (above 165 cc bikes could not be registered) [J]. TBS earlier reported the plan: allow production up to 500 cc but ban local use above 165 cc [K]. Whether imported high-cc bikes are now allowed was not stated in the article; treat as unresolved.
- A 2023 draft "Motorcycle Movement Policy" proposed barring bikes under 126 cc from highways and banning pillions on highways; it is a draft, status unconfirmed [L].
- Proposed 2026-27 budget advance income tax by cc (not a BRTA fee; collected at fitness renewal): up to 110 cc nil, 111-125 cc Tk 2,000, 126-165 cc Tk 5,000, above 165 cc Tk 10,000; reported 12 May 2026 as a proposal under review; final status unknown [M].

## 4. Number plates and RFID
- Act s.16(3): plate required. Retro-reflective number plate and RFID tag are mandatory; BRTA notice reported 24 June 2026 says owners who have not fitted them face "legal action under existing law" (no specific penalty or deadline) [N]. DMP announced action against vehicles without the BRTA-approved digital plate or working RFID, to start the following week (May 2026) [O].
- Front plates for motorbikes: government decision at a PM Office meeting on 18 July 2026; BRTA formed a seven-member review committee on 13 Aug 2026; size/placement/fees not yet decided (report 20 Aug 2026) [P].
- Fake plate: Act s.17/73 (6 months-2 years, Tk 1-5 lakh).

## 5. Tax token (ট্যাক্স টোকেন) and fitness
- Act s.26 requires a valid tax token; penalty up to Tk 10,000 (s.76). The road-tax rates by cc come from the Motor Vehicles Tax Act 1932 and its rules/SROs; I could not retrieve an authoritative per-cc table. A BRTA citizen-charter snippet showed Tk 518 for a "motorcycle/three-wheeler" tax token item but the document text was unclear and undated - NOT RELIABLE. Do not use.
- April 2024 extraordinary gazette extended deadlines for updating fitness, tax token, route permit and licences (details unread) [Q].
