# Bangladesh motorcycle traffic law - overview (মোটরসাইকেল ট্রাফিক আইন)

Access date: 2026-10-13. Not legal advice. Every figure has a citation in the linked file.

## Legal stack
1. **Road Transport Act 2018 (সড়ক পরিবহণ আইন, ২০১৮)** - Act 47 of 2018, in force 1 Nov 2019; replaced the Motor Vehicles Ordinance 1983 (s.125) - [road-transport-act-2018.md](road-transport-act-2018.md). Primary text: https://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-print-1262.html
2. **Road Transport Rules 2022 (সড়ক পরিবহণ বিধিমালা, ২০২২)** - S.R.O. 367/2022, gazette 27 Dec 2022; Schedule 1 fees, Schedule 2 driving test/rules/hand signals, Schedule 4 points - PDF: https://objectstorage.ap-dcc-gazipur-1.oraclecloud15.com/n/axvjbnqprylg/b/V2Ministry/o/office-rthd/2024/12/a4bd5ed71c014574ab29ebf0f6993fee.pdf (index: https://rthd.gov.bd/pages/laws). Bengal Motor Vehicles Rules 1940/1984 were repealed by the 2022 Rules (final rule, page 19614).
3. BRTA guidelines/notifications (speed 2024, ride sharing 2017, 375 cc 2023), Mobile Court Act 2009, Penal Code 1860 (accident deaths via s.105), CrPC 1898 procedure.

## 10-second rider summary
- Licence (18+, class 8, learner then smart card), registration certificate and number plate, tax token, fitness, plate/RFID: all required; fines are up to Tk 25,000-50,000 and up to 6 months jail for no licence/no registration.
- Helmet for rider AND pillion; one pillion only: s.49(1)(চ); penalty up to 3 months / Tk 10,000 / 1 point (s.92(1)). Mobile phone: up to 1 month / Tk 5,000 (s.92(2)).
- Speed: 30 km/h in cities for bikes; ~50 highways, 60 expressways (sources differ).
- Death/grievous injury by negligence: up to 5 years / Tk 5 lakh, non-bailable (s.105). Over-speed accident: 3 years / Tk 3 lakh (s.98).
- Police can fine on the spot, take the bike, file a mobile-court case; you get a receipt and charge copy.
- Banned: Padma Bridge (2022), Dhaka Elevated Expressway; election-day bans (Feb 2026).
