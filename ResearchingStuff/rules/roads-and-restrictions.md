# Roads, speed limits, lanes, bans (সড়ক, গতিসীমা, নিষিদ্ধ এলাকা)

Access date: 2026-10-13.

## 1. Speed limits - BRTA "Motor Vehicle Speed Limit Guideline 2024" (মোটরযানের গতিসীমা নির্দেশিকা ২০২৪)
Legal basis: Act s.44(1) (BRTA sets speed limits in consultation with road bodies); offence s.44(2), penalty s.87 (up to 3 months / Tk 10,000 / 1 point) [A].
TBS report of 10 May 2024 (announced Wednesday 8 May 2024) [B]:
| Road | Motorcycle | Other light vehicles | Trucks |
|---|---|---|---|
| Inside city corporations/municipal/district towns | 30 km/h | 40 km/h | 30 km/h |
| Expressway | 60 km/h | 80 km/h | 50 km/h |
| National highway (other than expressway) and district roads (as reported) | 50 km/h | 70-80 km/h (see below) | 45-50 km/h |
- Conflicts: TBS's table says motorcycle 50 km/h on "other national highways and district roads" and does not give the motorcycle figure for Category B roads; Dhaka Tribune's summary of the same guideline says 80 km/h for cars/buses/minibuses, 60 km/h for motorbikes, 50 km/h for trucks on highways; Observer says 60 on expressways, 50 on highways. Treat highway = 50 and expressway = 60 as best-supported (TBS, Observer) but unresolved; authoritative text not retrieved [B][C].
- Enforcement: BRTA spokesperson Mahbub-E-Rabbani said immediate enforcement was stated but wide implementation would be gradual, publicity first, mobile courts for speeding in the interim, speed guns for police [B]. Prothom Alo headline: "Motorcycles can't cross 30 km/h threshold in cities" [C2].
- Dhaka Elevated Expressway posted 60 km/h (Financial Express/Dhaka Tribune snippet); an earlier report expected speeds up to 80 km/h. Not verified from a notification [D].

## 2. Lane and overtaking rules (Rules 2022, Schedule 2(ক) "Rules for driving")
Read from gazette page 19621 (Bangla) [E]:
1. Drive in the assigned lane; keep to the left of the road ("রাস্তার বামদিক ঘেঁষিয়া"), and pass oncoming traffic on your right-hand side (opposite traffic goes to its own right).
2. Overtake on the right of the vehicle ahead normally; pass on its left only when that driver has signalled and is moving toward the middle to turn right.
3. Do not overtake when it inconveniences oncoming or other traffic, or where the road ahead is not clearly visible (bends, brows, hills, narrow bridge).
4. If someone behind is trying to overtake, you must not increase speed or block.
5. Slow down near junctions, road ends, bends; give way at main-road junctions; slow when you see crowds or unexpected obstructions; treat road workers with care.
(Paraphrase of the numbered items; the Bangla is the authority. Act: no dangerous/unauthorised overtaking s.44(3).)
Hand signals (Schedule 2(খ), page 19622) [E]: right turn - right arm extended horizontally outside; left turn - right arm outside, circling anticlockwise; slow down - right arm out, palm down, moved up and down several times; stop - right upper arm out, forearm raised, palm to the rear; overtake request (to vehicle behind) - right arm out and waved toward the direction. Wording paraphrased from the gazette; confirm in the Bangla before publishing.

## 3. Footpath, wrong way, parking
- No driving on the footpath adjacent to a road: s.49(1)(ঠ), penalty s.92(1). Wrong-side/opposite direction: s.49(1)(ঘ),(ঙ). Parking only in designated areas: s.47, s.90 (fine up to Tk 5,000 + 1 point) [A].

| Bangabandhu (Jamuna) Bridge, other bridges/expressways | No source found in this research - NOT VERIFIED either way | - |
| Dhaka-Chattogram Highway | Eid-time check posts have restricted bikes (riders pass with documents/necessity); permanent ban: none found | TBS snippet [G] |
| Election days | Election Commission letter: motorcycles banned 00:00 of 10 Feb to 00:00 of 13 Feb 2026 (72 h) for the 12 Feb 2026 national election and referendum; exemptions for law enforcement, press, emergency, EC-approved stickers/cards; no penalty stated | bdnews24 11 Feb 2026 [I] |
- Act s.46(4) explanation (vehicles unfit for roads: nosimon, karimon, vot-voti, easy-bike, motorised rickshaw/van) - not motorcycles [A].
- Draft only: 2023 Motorcycle Movement Policy would bar bikes under 126 cc from highways and pillions on highways [J].

## 5. Number of riders
One rider plus one pillion maximum (s.49(1)(চ)) [A].

## 6. Highway rules
- s.48 priority: higher-class highways have priority; fast vehicles joining. s.37: no illegal structures within 10 m of highway slope [A].
- A separate Highway Act (মহাসড়ক আইন) exists; I did not read it. Dhaka-Chattogram highway "auto fine" by AI camera launched 18 Aug 2026 by Highway Police: SMS to registered owner, pay by bKash/Nagad; offences named: speeding, prohibited parking, wrong-way driving; fine amounts and motorcycle coverage not stated [K].
