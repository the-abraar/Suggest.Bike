# Accidents, compensation, insurance (দুর্ঘটনা, ক্ষতিপূরণ, বিমা)

Access date: 2026-10-13.

## 1. What to do at an accident (Act s.62, s.119)
- Driver/conductor/representative must **immediately inform the nearest police station** and, as needed, fire service/hospital, and send the injured to the nearest hospital as fast as possible (s.62(1)). Failure: s.95 - up to 1 month, or up to Tk 20,000, or both, plus 1 point [A].
- Police must set up a nationwide toll-free number for accidents (s.62(2)); the national emergency number is 999 (DMP also cites 999) [B].
- Do not damage vehicles or attack the driver/crew after an accident; crowd offence s.103: up to 1 month / Tk 10,000 (s.119) [A].
- Grievous injury/death by reckless or negligent driving: s.105 - up to 5 years and/or up to Tk 5 lakh, non-bailable (s.117(1)(খ)); over-speed/loss of control: s.98 - up to 3 years / Tk 3 lakh, non-bailable; driver may be arrested without warrant (s.110(1)) [A].
- Good Samaritan: s.124(1)(ঢ) allows the government to give legal protection to first helpers; the specific protection is not in the Act text read (policy enabling clause only) [A].
- General Diary (জিডি) / FIR (এফআইআর): the Act does not use these words; CrPC 1898 applies (s.114(1)). GD is a police-station record for loss of licence/papers (the licence application form in the Rules asks for a GD copy for a lost licence, page 19672 of the gazette) [C]. For an accident with injury or death, an FIR/case is filed under s.105/98 (inference: police practice, not quoted from a source).

## 2. Compensation fund (আর্থিক সহায়তা তহবিল) - Act ss.52-61 [A]
- s.52: injured person or, if dead, the nominee of the heirs gets compensation or treatment cost from the Financial Assistance Fund as decided by the Trustee Board.
- s.53: the fund is financed by an annual or one-off contribution from each vehicle owner, rate set by rules per vehicle class; also paid at registration (s.16(2)). Non-payment: s.94 allows refusal of registration/fitness renewal or cancellation of permits plus a prescribed fine.
- s.54: Trustee Board (ট্রাস্টি বোর্ড) chaired by the BRTA chairman with ministry, highway police DIG, RHD, consumer rights, owners' association representatives, etc.
- s.59: apply to the Board chairman; chairman may arrange immediate treatment money; Board decides "equitable" compensation; amount criteria set by rules.
- s.61: if unhappy, apply for review within 30 days, then appeal to government within 30 days, then arbitration; civil courts cannot hear compensation claims under this chapter (s.61(4)).
- s.60(3): the fund does not pay for damage to the vehicle itself.
- Compensation amounts (taka) by death/injury grade: set by the Rules/Board - not extracted from the gazette; NOT VERIFIED.

## 3. Insurance (বিমা)
- Act s.60: (1) owner may insure passenger life/property for registered capacity; (2) owner "shall insure the vehicles in the proper manner" with vehicle damage covered; (4) terms by rules. The Prothom Alo/Jagonews reporting says the 2018 Act made vehicle (third-party risk) insurance effectively non-compulsory, while the earlier Motor Vehicles Ordinance 1983 had made third-party insurance compulsory [D][E].
- BRTA said in the past that no case can be filed for lack of insurance ("বীমা না থাকলেও মামলার সুযোগ নেই") [E]; Prothom Alo: "যানবাহনের বিমা না থাকলেও মামলা নয়" [E2]. Text not fetched (403 on Jagonews); relied on headlines/snippet.
- In October 2020 BRTA issued a notice; in December 2020 the insurance regulator IDRA withdrew the third-party risk product, and a replacement product was discussed but not finalised (Jagonews summary) [D].
- **Cabinet, 13 Mar 2024**: approved in principle a draft Road Transport (Amendment) Act 2024 making insurance mandatory for all vehicles including motorcycles; owners without cover would face a fine up to Tk 3,000; the state loses about Tk 878 crore a year (Tk 849 crore tax/VAT, Tk 28 crore stamp duty) from uninsured vehicles [F]. No evidence of enactment found; treat third-party cover as legally uncertain and practically optional.
- Documents police may ask for: licence, registration certificate (blue book), tax token, fitness, insurance certificate if held; s.111 mentions an "insurance certificate" among papers examined [A]; route permit only for commercial.

## 4. Practical notes
- Hit-and-run: no separate section; falls under s.62/95 (failure to inform/help) and s.98/105 plus Penal Code (inference).
- Claiming for your own damage: only via a private comprehensive policy (s.60(2),(3)); fund excludes vehicle damage.
