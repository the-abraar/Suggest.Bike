# Road Transport Act 2018 (সড়ক পরিবহণ আইন, ২০১৮) - motorbike-relevant sections

Access date for all citations: 2026-10-13. Primary text: Bangla (authoritative; s.126(2) says Bangla prevails over any English text) at bdlaws, print view [A]. Act No. 47 of 2018, assented 8 Oct 2018, in force 1 Nov 2019 by S.R.O. 333-Law/2019 dated 22 Oct 2019 (footnote to s.1 in [A]). It repealed the Motor Vehicles Ordinance 1983 (s.125(1)); old rules under the Ordinance stay in force until new rules replace them (s.125(4)). The Road Transport Rules 2022 were gazetted 27 Dec 2022 (S.R.O. 367/2022) [B].

Reading note: the bdlaws print view shows the current consolidated text as of 2026-10-13 and still shows the original penalties (e.g. s.85 fine up to Tk 10,000). The 2024 amendment bill (cabinet approval in principle 13 Mar 2024) is NOT reflected; I found no evidence it became law (see recent-changes-and-news.md). One low-quality site (allresultbd) mentions a "Road Transport Act, 2025"; I could not verify this anywhere official and treat it as wrong/unconfirmed.

## Key definitions (s.2)
- Motor cycle / মোটর সাইকেল (s.2(46)): "a two-wheeled motor vehicle" (দুই চাকা বিশিষ্ট মোটরযান) [A]. No cc threshold in the Act.
- Motor vehicle / মোটরযান (s.2(42)); driver / চালক (s.2(44)); owner / মালিক (s.2(45)); accident / দুর্ঘটনা (s.2(20)); demerit point / দোষসূচক পয়েন্ট (s.2(21)); fitness certificate / ফিটনেস সনদ (s.2(34)); tax token / ট্যাক্স টোকেন (s.2(15)); traffic sign / ট্রাফিক সাইন (s.2(14), refers to the "National Traffic Sign Manual"); no-horn zone / নীরব এলাকা (s.2(25)); contract carriage / কন্ট্রাক্ট ক্যারিজ (s.2(8)) expressly includes "motor cycle" hired for a fare, i.e. a rental/ride-hailing bike is treated as a contract carriage [A].

## Commercial use / route permit (ss.28, 31)
- s.28(1): public-place use of a "transport vehicle" requires a route permit (রুট পারমিট); penalty s.77: up to 3 months, or up to Tk 20,000, or both [A]. Applies to commercial/hire vehicles, not private bikes.
- s.31(1): no commercial activity with a vehicle to which route permit does not apply, except special permission by BRTA; the Explanation says the "special commercial activity" is hiring out a private vehicle on prescribed conditions. Penalty s.79: up to 3 months, or up to Tk 25,000, or both, plus 1 demerit point for a driver [A]. This is the legal hook for unregistered ride-hailing bikes (inference, flagged: the Act text does not name ride-sharing here).
- s.124(1)(ট): government may issue orders/policy on ride sharing; s.124(1)(ণ): carrier use for transporting two-wheelers etc. [A].

## Vehicle construction/modification (s.40)
- 40(3): no change from BRTA technical specifications to dimensions, wheelbase, wheel size, brakes, steering, horn, safety glass, signalling lights and reflectors, speed governor, exhaust emissions or noise level. 40(4): any technical/internal/external modification of a registered vehicle needs BRTA approval. Penalty s.84: 1-3 years jail (minimum 1 year) or up to Tk 3 lakh, or both; non-bailable [A]. Relevant to exhaust/handlebar/lighting mods (inference).

## Traffic control (ss.41-49)
- s.42: obey traffic signs and signals; pedestrians use zebra/foot-overbridge; any uniformed police officer or vehicle inspector may order a driver to stop. Penalty s.85: up to 1 month, or up to Tk 10,000, or both, plus 1 point [A].
- s.43: axle/laden weight limits. Penalty s.86: up to 1 year or Tk 1 lakh, 2 points.
- s.44: speed - BRTA sets limits by road class (44(1)); no exceeding limit or reckless driving (44(2)); no dangerous or unauthorised overtaking or obstructing (44(3)). Penalty s.87: up to 3 months, or up to Tk 10,000, or both, plus 1 point.
- s.45: noise - govt sets noise level; no excess noise; no horn in declared silent zones; no fitting loud horns/devices. Penalty s.88: up to 3 months, or up to Tk 10,000, or both, plus 1 point.
- s.46: polluting emissions; no defective/banned vehicles (explanation lists nosimon, karimon, vot-voti, easy-bike, motorised rickshaw/van - not motorcycles). Penalty s.89(1): up to 3 months, or up to Tk 25,000 (+1 point); s.89(2) (s.46(4)): up to 3 months, or up to Tk 20,000 (+1 point).
- s.47: park only in designated areas; stopping only at designated pick-up points. Penalty s.90: fine up to Tk 5,000 plus 1 point.
- s.48: highway priority rules. Penalty s.91: up to 1 month, or up to Tk 5,000, plus 1 point.
- **s.49(1) general instructions - the core for riders** [A]:
  - Part 1: (ক) no driving after alcohol/intoxicants; (ঘ) no driving against the prescribed direction on roads; (ঙ) no stopping at a non-designated place or wrong side causing congestion; **(চ) "no more than one pillion passenger on a motorcycle besides the driver, and both driver and pillion must properly wear helmets"** (চালক ব্যতীত মোটরসাইকেলে একজনের অধিক সহযাত্রী বহন করা যাইবে না এবং চালক ও সহযাত্রী উভয়কে যথাযথভাবে হেলমেট ব্যবহার করিতে হইবে); (ছ) no boarding/alighting while moving; (ঝ) no cargo/passengers outside the body; (ঠ) no vehicle on the footpath adjoining road; (ড) no taking a vehicle out without the owner's/lawful permission. Penalty s.92(1): up to 3 months, or up to Tk 10,000, or both, plus 1 point.
  - Part 2: (ক) **no mobile phone or similar equipment while driving**; (খ) seat belt; (জ) no high beam that disturbs oncoming traffic at night. Penalty s.92(2): up to 1 month, or up to Tk 5,000, or both, plus 1 point.
  - 49(2): government may amend the instructions by gazette.
- s.51: explosives/inflammables. s.93: up to 3 months/Tk 25,000.

## Accidents, compensation, insurance (ss.52-62)
See accidents-and-insurance.md. Summary: Financial Assistance Fund (আর্থিক সহায়তা তহবিল) s.53 and Trustee Board (ট্রাস্টি বোর্ড) s.54 pay compensation/treatment for accident victims s.52; owners contribute annually or one-off at registration; insurance s.60 is worded as the owner "shall insure in the proper manner" (60(2)) but 60(1) says passenger life insurance "if wish"; details to rules; s.62 duty to inform police/hospital.

## Offences, powers and procedure (ss.66-119)
- s.98: death/property loss from over-speed, reckless driving, dangerous overtaking, overloading or loss of control: up to 3 years, or up to Tk 3 lakh, or both; court may direct fine paid to the victim; non-bailable (s.117(1)(খ)).
- s.100: repeat offence: up to double the maximum, and not less than double the previous sentence.
- **s.105: death or grievous injury through driving - offences are treated under the Penal Code 1860; proviso: for reckless/negligent driving causing grievous hurt or death, up to 5 years, or up to Tk 5 lakh, or both (notwithstanding Penal Code s.304B)**; non-bailable.
- s.99: abetment/conspiracy = same punishment.
- s.108: police officer of SI/sergeant rank or above (or authorised BRTA inspector) frames a "charge" for offences under ss.43, 66, 72, 75, 84, 87, 89, 92, 95; a copy goes to the accused who signs or thumbprints; s.108(2) officer may impose a "reasonable fine" payable by a date at a designated place, and no further action follows if paid; s.108(4) refusing the copy or receipt = arrest without warrant and an extra fine up to Tk 5,000 on conviction.
- s.109: such officers may demand a driver stop and produce papers.
- s.110: uniformed police may arrest without warrant for offences under ss.72, 73, 75, 77, 79, 84, 86, 89, 92(1), 98, 105 committed in their presence; they must secure the vehicle or take it to the nearest police station and inform the owner within 24 hours.
- s.111: police may seize papers believed forged; may seize a driver's licence if the driver may flee, in which case a temporary receipt lets the driver keep driving until the date on it.
- s.112: consequences of driving without registration/fitness/route permit (text partly not read).
- s.113: in areas notified by BRTA, SI/sergeant+ may impose on-the-spot fines for offences under ss.43, 66, 72, 75, 84, 87, 89, 92, 95; officer records offence and amount on a prescribed form and gives a receipt; if the driver refuses the copy or does not pay, the vehicle goes to the nearest police station and is released after payment.
- s.114(1): CrPC 1898 applies to investigation/trial; **s.114(2): offences are listed in the Schedule of the Mobile Court Act 2009 so an executive magistrate can try them**.
- s.115: SI/sergeant (traffic) or authorised official may seize a vehicle; executive magistrate or inspector/sergeant may send it to a dumping yard (ডাম্পিং ইয়ার্ড); owner may apply for release within 2 months (to the court for mobile court cases, otherwise to SP/DC police); unclaimed vehicles can be handed to a public body or auctioned with court permission.
- s.117: all offences are cognizable if an SI/sergeant+ or BRTA inspector knows of them; bailable except ss.84, 98, 105; compoundable: ss.66, 72, 75, 87, 89, 92 (a judicial magistrate, authorised BRTA officer or officer of at least additional SP rank may settle).
- s.119: after an accident the vehicle must not be damaged and no mob violence against driver is allowed (penalty s.103: up to 1 month, or up to Tk 10,000, or both).
- s.118/102: failing to obey lawful orders/provide information: up to 1 month, or up to Tk 10,000.
- s.104: breach of government orders under s.124: up to 3 months, or up to Tk 20,000.

## Uncertain / conflicting
