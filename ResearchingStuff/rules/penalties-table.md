# Penalties table for motorcyclists (দণ্ড / জরিমানা)

Access date: 2026-10-13. Source of every row: Road Transport Act 2018 (RTA), bdlaws print view [A] - offence section, penalty section and points; "Pts" = demerit points deducted from the 12 on a licence (Act s.11; Rules Schedule 4 [B]). "Up to" = "অনধিক" (maximum, courts may impose less). Figures are statutory maximums; mobile-court/on-the-spot fines are "reasonable" amounts set by the officer (s.108(2), s.113) and no official per-offence fine schedule was found - sites quoting "Tk 1,000 helmet fine" are unofficial (flagged below).

IMPORTANT caveat: draft amendment (cabinet nod 13 Mar 2024) would have cut several of these (e.g. s.85 to Tk 2,000, s.81 to 3 months/Tk 25,000). I found no evidence it was enacted; the bdlaws text still shows the original figures [A][C].

## D. What happens on the spot
1. Police (SI/sergeant+) or authorised BRTA inspector can stop you and check papers (ss.42(4), 109).
2. For ss.43, 66, 72, 75, 84, 87, 89, 92, 95 they frame a "charge" (অভিযোগ), give you a copy to sign/thumbprint (s.108(1)) and may fix a "reasonable fine" (s.108(2)); in BRTA-notified areas they may fine on the spot with a receipt (s.113).
3. If you refuse the copy or do not pay, the bike can be sent to the nearest police station (s.113(3)) and released on payment; alternatively dumping yard, with 2 months to apply for release (s.115).
4. Mobile court (executive magistrate) can try offences because the Act is in the Mobile Court Act 2009 schedule (s.114(2)).
5. Licence may be held with a temporary receipt (s.111(2),(3)).

## Unofficial figures to ignore unless confirmed
- Sites (e.g. banglarbike, allresultbd) quote helmet fines "from Tk 1,000" and "min Tk 5,000, max Tk 5 lakh" under a "2025 Act"; I found no primary-law support. Flagged as unverified [D].
- Media in Nov 2019 described the Act's whole range as "Tk 5,000 to Tk 5 lakh fines, 1 month to 5 years jail" (Prothom Alo) - consistent with the table above but not itself a per-offence list [E].
