## Data-quality caveats (read first)
- bikebd.com is an aggregator. Its own disclaimer says its info "may not be 100 percent accurate" (page text, bikebd.com/price/*, accessed 2026-10-13). Some pages carry stale prices for discontinued variants, "mileage claimed" and "user mileage" are often the same copied number (so they are NOT independent real-world data), seat-height values like 1,085 mm / 1,133 mm / 1,250 mm on some pages are clearly mis-entered, and "Made In / Assemble In" fields are sometimes N/A. Treat any bikebd-only number as UNVERIFIED unless an official source is cited next to it.
- Prices are showroom/list prices and move often; the "(Oct 2026)" in a bikebd title is the page's own header date, not proof the price was checked that month. Access date for every URL in this file: 2026-10-13.
- Claimed mileage is the maker's or aggregator's number. Real-world owner figures are given only where a cited owner report exists.

## Official / news anchors
- TVS Apache RTR 160 4V (carb, single disc) launched in BD with press release dated 8 April 2019 together with Max 125, Metro special edition, XL100 i-Touch Start: 159.7 cc, 16.5 PS @8000, 14.8 Nm @6500, 5-speed, 113 km/h top speed, 0-60 in 4.73 s, colours RR red/racing blue/racing black, sold via TVS Auto Bangladesh showrooms ([TVS Motor press release](https://www.tvsmotor.com/media/press-release/tvs-motor-company-launches-4-new-products-in-bangladesh), 2026-10-13). Note bikebd lists top speed 130 km/h for the same model - conflicting (113 official vs 130 aggregator).
- TVS collapse: 2024 sales 29,932 (-35%); 2025 just over 10,000 (-80%) vs typical ~60,000; H2-2025 3,590 vs 11,947. CEO Biplob Kumar Roy: 'It's not the market, it's us' - no new/upgraded models ([Daily Star 16 Jan 2026](https://www.thedailystar.net/news/japanese-brands-take-the-lead-motorcycle-market-recovers-4082151), [Daily Star 18 Jan 2025](https://www.thedailystar.net/business/economy/news/motorcycle-sales-hit-six-year-low-3802466), 2026-10-13). TVS Auto Bangladesh is a JV of TVS & Sons and Rian Motors (TVS international page via search summary).
- TVS was therefore NOT a top seller in 2025 despite being on many 'best' lists (bikebd 2022 list names Apache RTR 160 4V and Raider 125).
