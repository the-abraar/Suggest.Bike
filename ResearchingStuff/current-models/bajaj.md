# Bajaj (Bangladesh) - current models

Distributor: Uttara Motors Ltd per Bajaj's Bangladesh pages; the N160 page says assembled in Bangladesh ([Bajaj BD blog page via search summary](https://bajajauto.com/en-bd/blogs/blog-pulsar-n160), 2026-10-13 - only seen as a search-result summary, UNVERIFIED by direct read).

Primary source: official [bajajauto.com/en-bd](https://www.bajajauto.com/en-bd/bikes) product pages fetched 2026-10-13. Every row links its own page. Official pages give no mileage claim or top speed in the specification block (some FAQ text exists but was not extracted). Weight = 'Kerb Weight' as printed.

**Warning about Bajaj's own pages:** the page titled `pulsar-n160-td` lists the engine as 'Carburettor' but brakes as ABS; `pulsar-150-td` says 'air cooled' and 'Single Channel' front brake type while `pulsar-150-td-abs` says 'oil cooled' - Bajaj's copy is inconsistent between variant pages. Treat fuel-system/cooling of Pulsar 150 and N160 variants as needing dealer confirmation.

## Market context
- Bajaj was #1 brand in 2024: 85,696 units (-10%), 21.8% share ([Daily Star, 18 Jan 2025](https://www.thedailystar.net/business/economy/news/motorcycle-sales-hit-six-year-low-3802466), 2026-10-13). In 2025 Bajaj slid to 15.7% (TBS) or 17.6% (Daily Star) - two reports disagree, see market overview. ([TBS](https://www.tbsnews.net/node/1479086), [Daily Star](https://www.thedailystar.net/news/japanese-brands-take-the-lead-motorcycle-market-recovers-4082151)).
