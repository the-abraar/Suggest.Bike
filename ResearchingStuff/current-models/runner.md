## Data-quality caveats (read first)
- bikebd.com is an aggregator. Its own disclaimer says its info "may not be 100 percent accurate" (page text, bikebd.com/price/*, accessed 2026-10-13). Some pages carry stale prices for discontinued variants, "mileage claimed" and "user mileage" are often the same copied number (so they are NOT independent real-world data), seat-height values like 1,085 mm / 1,133 mm / 1,250 mm on some pages are clearly mis-entered, and "Made In / Assemble In" fields are sometimes N/A. Treat any bikebd-only number as UNVERIFIED unless an official source is cited next to it.
- Prices are showroom/list prices and move often; the "(Oct 2026)" in a bikebd title is the page's own header date, not proof the price was checked that month. Access date for every URL in this file: 2026-10-13.
- Claimed mileage is the maker's or aggregator's number. Real-world owner figures are given only where a cited owner report exists.

- Runner Automobiles: local maker and also KTM distributor in BD per bikebd; began small-scale exports (Nepal, Bhutan per TBS July 2026; Daily Star Dec 2025). Electric brands named: Runner among Walton, Komaki, Green Tiger, Akij ([Daily Star](https://www.thedailystar.net/supplements/the-long-ride/news/the-next-25-years-where-bangladeshs-motorcycle-industry-headed-4068991), 2026-10-13). No Runner unit sales appear in the 2024/2025 brand tables found; Runner is not in the Daily Star/TBS top-five lists, so share is below the 'other' remainder (top five >93%, so Runner+all others < 7%, Daily Star Jan 2026).
- Owner/editorial notes (bikebd summaries, via search): Bullet 100 cons vibration, weak headlight, brakes; Bullet 100 V2 cons weak rear suspension, less powerful engine; Turbo 125 V2 cons halogen headlight, thin tyres, 133 kg weight. Ratings from ~9 reviews each, reads like editorial summary; no real-world mileage test found ([bikebd Turbo V2](https://www.bikebd.com/price/runner-turbo-v2/specifications), 2026-10-13). Price conflict for Turbo 125 V2: 132,000 vs 155,000.
