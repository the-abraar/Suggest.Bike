## Data-quality caveats (read first)
- bikebd.com is an aggregator. Its own disclaimer says its info "may not be 100 percent accurate" (page text, bikebd.com/price/*, accessed 2026-10-13). Some pages carry stale prices for discontinued variants, "mileage claimed" and "user mileage" are often the same copied number (so they are NOT independent real-world data), seat-height values like 1,085 mm / 1,133 mm / 1,250 mm on some pages are clearly mis-entered, and "Made In / Assemble In" fields are sometimes N/A. Treat any bikebd-only number as UNVERIFIED unless an official source is cited next to it.
- Prices are showroom/list prices and move often; the "(Oct 2026)" in a bikebd title is the page's own header date, not proof the price was checked that month. Access date for every URL in this file: 2026-10-13.
- Claimed mileage is the maker's or aggregator's number. Real-world owner figures are given only where a cited owner report exists.

## Official / news anchors
- New XBlade unveiled 6 Feb 2024 at Tk 195,000; 160cc HET engine, claimed 59 km/L, petal disc, LED lamps, digital meter with gear indicator; colours sports red, matte steel metallic, strontium silver ([Prothom Alo English](https://en.prothomalo.com/amp/story/corporate/local/l1cjysmr2m), 2026-10-13). bikebd now lists X-Blade 160 Fi ABS at 235,000 (ABS variant) and 45 km/L; the 59 vs 45 gap = Honda claim vs aggregator figure, no independent test found.
- Honda exports: 32 XBlade to Mexico (May, per TBS July 2026 article) after Guatemala shipments (14 units in 2024 per Daily Star Dec 2025) ([TBS](https://www.tbsnews.net/node/1479086), [Daily Star](https://www.thedailystar.net/supplements/the-long-ride/news/the-next-25-years-where-bangladeshs-motorcycle-industry-headed-4068991)).
- Distributor/assembler: Bangladesh Honda Private Limited (BHPL), per bikebd; Honda's own motorcycle site could not be located (honda.com.bd is the cars/DHS Motors site).
- Honda sales: 2024 15.2% share, slight fall (Daily Star 18 Jan 2025); 2025 84,813 units about 20% (TBS) or 17.8% (Daily Star) - conflicting bases. Chief marketing officer quote: 'Our priority remains meeting the needs of the domestic market.' (TBS).
- Owner notes (X-Blade 160, bikebd user blogs via search summary): hard gear shift before first service that eased with break-in; early power loss after first 300 km oil change; 75-90 km/h highway stable with no vibration; one owner paid Tk 172,900 at Baridhara, no measured real-world mileage in any report ([4500 km](https://bikebd.com/blog/honda-xblade-160-4500-km-user-review-imtiaz), [1500 km](https://bikebd.com/blog/honda-xblade-160-1500-km-user-review-romel-khan), 2026-10-13).
- Shine 100: bikebd shows 111,000 BDT (Shine 100) and 117,000 (Shine 100 DX); spec 98.98 cc, 7.28 bhp @7500, 8.05 Nm @5000 (bikebd, via search summary); claimed mileage 55-60 km/L (bikebd/FasterWheeler) conflicts with the 40 Kmpl on bikebd's DX page; Shine 100 DX page lists 'made India / asm N/A' - CBU vs local assembly UNVERIFIED.
