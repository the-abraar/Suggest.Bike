## Data-quality caveats (read first)
- bikebd.com is an aggregator. Its own disclaimer says its info "may not be 100 percent accurate" (page text, bikebd.com/price/*, accessed 2026-10-13). Some pages carry stale prices for discontinued variants, "mileage claimed" and "user mileage" are often the same copied number (so they are NOT independent real-world data), seat-height values like 1,085 mm / 1,133 mm / 1,250 mm on some pages are clearly mis-entered, and "Made In / Assemble In" fields are sometimes N/A. Treat any bikebd-only number as UNVERIFIED unless an official source is cited next to it.
- Prices are showroom/list prices and move often; the "(Oct 2026)" in a bikebd title is the page's own header date, not proof the price was checked that month. Access date for every URL in this file: 2026-10-13.
- Claimed mileage is the maker's or aggregator's number. Real-world owner figures are given only where a cited owner report exists.
