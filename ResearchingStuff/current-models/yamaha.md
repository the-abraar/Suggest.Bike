### Official-page oddities
- FZS V2 (13.2 PS / 12.8 Nm) vs FZS V4 / FZ-X / FZS Fi Hybrid (12.4 PS / 13.3 Nm): the newer (E20-compliant) tune has LESS peak power; the official pages themselves show this. bikebd lists the V4 at 12.23 bhp and V2 at 13 bhp, consistent.
- MT-15 V1/V2 and R15 V3 official pages show 19.3 PS @10,000 (the older tune); R15 V4/R15M show 18.4 PS @10,000 / 14.2 Nm. The R15 V3 'Indian Version' is still sold per bikebd (525,000).
- Saluto 125 UBS page markets 'Mileage 78+ Kmph' (sic) and 'Longest Seat (680 MM)': marketing text, unit 'Kmph' is likely a typo for km/L; UNVERIFIED claim. Ground clearance 180 mm claimed on the same page.
- The Ray ZR pages state Unified Braking System; Ray ZR 125 FI + Street Rally display 'Smart Motor Generator', 'Stop & Start'.

## Data-quality caveats (read first)
- bikebd.com is an aggregator. Its own disclaimer says its info "may not be 100 percent accurate" (page text, bikebd.com/price/*, accessed 2026-10-13). Some pages carry stale prices for discontinued variants, "mileage claimed" and "user mileage" are often the same copied number (so they are NOT independent real-world data), seat-height values like 1,085 mm / 1,133 mm / 1,250 mm on some pages are clearly mis-entered, and "Made In / Assemble In" fields are sometimes N/A. Treat any bikebd-only number as UNVERIFIED unless an official source is cited next to it.
- Prices are showroom/list prices and move often; the "(Oct 2026)" in a bikebd title is the page's own header date, not proof the price was checked that month. Access date for every URL in this file: 2026-10-13.
- Claimed mileage is the maker's or aggregator's number. Real-world owner figures are given only where a cited owner report exists.

## Launch / market
- Daily Star's calendar-2025 count shows Suzuki 20.1% / Yamaha 19.7% for the full year, with Yamaha leading H2 2025 at 22% share; TBS's differently-based count gives Yamaha 88,289 units, 20.8% ([Daily Star](https://www.thedailystar.net/news/japanese-brands-take-the-lead-motorcycle-market-recovers-4082151), [TBS](https://www.tbsnews.net/node/1479086), 2026-10-13). 82% of Yamaha buyers are existing motorcycle users and 55% upgraded from Indian brands (Daily Star, quoting ACI Motors). 2024: +11%, 19.3% share (Daily Star 18 Jan 2025). First hybrid motorcycle in Bangladesh: FZS Fi Hybrid per Yamaha BD site banner ([yamahabd.com](https://www.yamahabd.com/products/yamaha-fzs-fi-hybrid/), 2026-10-13).
