Other structural numbers:
- Capacity about 800,000 units/yr, domestic demand about 500,000, locally made share "nearly 99%" vs over 95% imported a decade ago (TBS). Daily Star Dec 2025: 800,000-1,000,000 capacity, Tk 6,000 crore invested; TBS: Tk 10,000 crore, about 200,000 jobs, 10 plants (DS says seven full plants in a search summary - CONFLICT, not resolved).
- Over 700 parts per bike imported; only chains, seats, stands, batteries made locally (both outlets).
- CKD duty cut by 25 points to 20% in FY17 (TBS) - the policy that enabled local assembly.
- Price band Tk 80,000 to Tk 5 lakh = 4-25 months of average income; City Bank "City Bike Loan" launched 2024, up to 80% financing (DS Dec 2025).
- Electric: EV motorcycle imports 2,446 (2022-23) to 10,053 (2024-25); only 261 registered electric two-wheelers (DS Dec 2025).

Honda's CMO says competing with Indian makers is "extremely difficult" under current duties (TBS).

## 3. Registrations (BRTA)
- New motorcycle registrations in 2024: 2.62 lakh, lowest since 2015, about half the 2022 peak; total vehicle registrations -15% ([AIR/newsonair summary of BRTA report, 4 Jan 2025](https://www.newsonair.gov.in/bangladesh-vehicle-registration-drops-15-to-a-decade-low-in-2024), via search summary - not re-read).
- Registered stock: 26.23 lakh motorbikes of 40.18 lakh vehicles at an older date (about 2022) (Daily Star via search summary); 43 lakh+ registered motorcycle riders Jan 2024 (search summary). Dhaka: 1,068,866 motorcycles of 2,029,979 vehicles (Dhaka Tribune, year unclear).
- BRTA gives no brand/model split in anything I found. Model-level registration data: NOT FOUND.
- Sales (about 385k-390k in 2024) exceeds BRTA new registrations (262k) - the gap is not explained in any source; treat BRTA numbers as registrations completed within the year, not sales.
