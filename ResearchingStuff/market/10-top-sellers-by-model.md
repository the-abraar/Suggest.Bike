# Likely top-selling models in Bangladesh (2024-2026) - evidence-based shortlist

**Important: no source with model-level unit sales was found.** Brand-level shares are in 00-market-overview.md. The list below ranks models only by the weak evidence that exists and says so. Do not quote the order as measured sales. All URLs accessed 2026-10-13.

## What evidence exists
1. bikebd.com's "Most Selling Bike In Bangladesh" article (updated 8 Sep 2022 per the page; table of figures was empty) names, without numbers: Suzuki Gixxer, Honda X-Blade 160, Yamaha FZS FI Double Disc, Yamaha R15 V3 Indian Dual ABS, Lifan KPR 165R EFI, TVS Apache RTR 160 4V, Hero Hunk Glossy, TVS Raider 125 ([bikebd](https://www.bikebd.com/info/most-selling-bike-in-bangladesh)). Dated 2022, pre-dates TVS's collapse.
2. Press mentions: Honda X-Blade is the only model named in the 2025-26 market stories (exports to Guatemala 2024, 32 to Mexico in May 2026; [TBS](https://www.tbsnews.net/node/1479086), [DS](https://www.thedailystar.net/supplements/the-long-ride/news/the-next-25-years-where-bangladeshs-motorcycle-industry-headed-4068991)); the Daily Star Jan 2026 piece mentions only "the 150cc segment" and no model.
3. Brand strength (shares) and the product ranges each brand lists as current - the segment the brand sells most is not stated anywhere.
4. Stock flags on Suzuki's site (units remaining) are NOT sales: Gixxer SF default colour stockRemaining=0 on 2026-10-13, Gixxer 250 = 17 ([Suzuki API](https://api-v2.suzuki.com.bd/api/user/products)).
