# Official motorcycle engine oil distributors in Bangladesh

Checked 2026-10-25. Every fact below comes from a page that was actually opened. The full sources are in `oil-distributors-bd.json`.

## Shell (Shell Advance): Ranks Petroleum Limited (RkPL), a RANCON company. Confidence: high
- **Brand-owner proof:** Shell's own distributor locator (shell.com, Bangladesh entry) names Ranks Petroleum Limited.
  - Corporate office: 387 Tejgaon I/A, Dhaka 1208.
  - Registered address: Ulukhola, Kaliganj, Gazipur.
  - General query line: +880 1787-672203.
- **What RkPL says:** its site calls it the "Macro Distributor of Shell Lubricants in Bangladesh" and says the partnership dates from 2004. Dhaka Tribune (Mar 2025) says "sole macro distributor".
- **Contact:** hotline 16775, info@shellbd.com, support@rkpl.com.bd. Site: rkpl.com.bd.
- **Spotting fakes:** RkPL's "Shell BD" Android app advertises "one-tap product authenticity checking". No Bangladesh page found explains how it works (QR code? scratch code?).

## Motul: RANCON (exclusive distributor). Confidence: medium
- **News proof:** The Business Standard and Dhaka Tribune (both 1 Aug 2026) report that Motul appointed Rancon exclusive distributor for bikes, cars and commercial vehicles. BikeBD says the launch was 27 Jul 2026 and that Motul India handed certificates to dealers.
- **Distributor's own site:** estore.bdmotul.com says "RANCON Group, the exclusive official distributor".
  - Contact: support@bdmotul.com, +880 9617357735.
  - The app listing gives the address as 387 Tejgaon I/A, Dhaka 1208, with developer Ranks Petroleum Ltd.
- **Spotting fakes:** scan the authentication QR code on the product with the "Motul Bangladesh" app (Google Play `com.motulbd.consumer`). Source: the FAQ on the estore.
- **Why only medium:** motul.com has no Bangladesh page (`/en-BD` redirects to France), so no brand-owner page confirms this.

## Mobil (ExxonMobil, incl. Mobil Super Moto): MJL Bangladesh PLC. Confidence: high
- **Brand-owner proof:** mobil.com's distributor finder lists MJL BANGLADESH PLC, but only as an "Industrial lubricants distributor".
  - Address: Mobil House, CWS (A) 13/A Gulshan Avenue, Dhaka 1212.
  - Contact: +88 02 226601429, enquiry@mobilbd.com.
- **What MJL says:** "strategic alliance partner of ExxonMobil in Bangladesh". The Mobil trademark is used under licence. The Mobil Super Moto range is on its motorcycle oils page. TBS (2020) calls it ExxonMobil's "authorised manufacturer and dealer".
- **Contact:** hotline 16669. Consumer shop: buymobil.mjlbl.com (buymobil@mjlbl.com).
- **Spotting fakes** (buymobil.mjlbl.com/security-seal): check the new security seal.
  1. Tilt the can. The MJL mark changes from silver to gold and a tick appears.
  2. The GEAR graphic changes from orange to bright green.
  3. Scan the seal's QR code with the REVEO Scanner app.

  There is also a ring-peel liner under the cap that can't be resealed.

## For all three
- **Trade licence:** not publicly verifiable.
- **Wholesale terms** (minimum order, dealer application): not published, contact required.

## What could not be confirmed
- **Motul:** no brand-owner page (motul.com) lists a Bangladesh distributor.
- **Motul:** the exact Rancon legal entity is unclear. The app is published by Ranks Petroleum Ltd, TBS tags Rancon Auto Industries Ltd, and rancon.com.bd links Motul to motoindustries.com.bd, which is an empty directory.
- **Motul:** Royal Enterprise (Rajshahi) says on its own site that it was Motul's exclusive distributor from 2020. No brand-owner or news source backs this, and it conflicts with the 2026 Rancon appointment. It was excluded.
- **Shell:** shell.com.bd did not resolve.
- **Shell:** how the Shell BD app verifies a product is not published. Shell's QR / Laava / SMS-8042 check is documented only for Pakistan and was not assumed to apply in Bangladesh.
- **Mobil:** mobil.com lists MJL only as an *industrial* distributor. The consumer and motorcycle role is backed by MJL's licence statement and by TBS, not by the mobil.com listing itself.
- **Mobil:** the DSE company page for MJLBD returned HTTP 410, so the stock listing was not verified.
- **Retailer and dealer locators:** the pages on rkpl.com.bd and buymobil.mjlbl.com need JavaScript and returned no data.
