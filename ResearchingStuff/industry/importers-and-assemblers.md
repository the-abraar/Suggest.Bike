## Industry facts
- Annual sales ~270,000 (2016), ~387,000 (2017), ~480,000 (2018); Jan-Jun 2025 257,632 units vs 202,330 a year earlier. Motorcycle Industry Development Policy 2018 cut CKD duty by 25 points to 20%. Seven companies run full manufacturing plants. Hero plant 125,000 units/yr. [Daily Star](https://www.thedailystar.net/supplements/long-ride/news/how-bangladesh-became-motorcycle-nation-4068976)
- Other background: [Automotive industry in Bangladesh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Automotive_industry_in_Bangladesh), [Bajaj plant](https://www.thedailystar.net/bajaj-to-make-motorcycles-at-bangladesh-plant-38123) (not read in detail).
- Note: tables above come from BikeBD listing/profile pages, which contradict each other in places; confirm against brand's own site before shipping.
