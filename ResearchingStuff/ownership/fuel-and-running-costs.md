## Registration and tax (cross-reference only)
- Registration ~Tk9,291 (50-125cc) and ~Tk11,764 (above 125cc), plus periodic road tax. (Dhaka Tribune 6 Jun 2026: https://www.dhakatribune.com/amp/business/411857/nbr-drops-direct-tax-on-autorickshaws)
- Feb 2021 registration-fee cut: up to 100cc Tk2,000 (was 4,200), over 100cc Tk3,000 (was 5,600); bundled with inspection and road tax with two-year validity. (Daily Star: https://www.thedailystar.net/business/news/bike-registration-fee-halved-2038333). TBS 2020 proposal all-in: Tk8,579 (<=100cc) / Tk11,590 (>100cc). The 2021 and 2026 totals differ by era; UNCERTAIN which is current.
