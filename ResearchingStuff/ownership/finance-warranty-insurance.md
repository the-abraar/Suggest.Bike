## Insurance
- Third-party "Motor Liability Insurance": not mandatory (abolished Dec 2020 under Road Transport Act 2018); IDRA relaunched Aug 2025 as one-year pilot; 150cc motorcycle total annual premium Tk1,006; cover: death/permanent total disability up to Tk2,00,000, serious injury Tk20,000, property Tk60,000, legal Tk10,000. Uptake minimal. (TBS 10 Feb 2026: https://www.tbsnews.net/node/1357826)
- A pending amendment to re-mandate insurance (fine up to Tk3,000) was reported, undated; status UNVERIFIED. (Prothom Alo: https://en.prothomalo.com/bangladesh/gzl00ewg0c)
- Comprehensive bike insurance (own-damage/theft) products and premiums by insurer: NOT FOUND.
