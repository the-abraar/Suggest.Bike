# Touring and road knowledge

Access date for all citations: 2026-10-13. Weak coverage: this file is mostly gaps.

## Padma Bridge / expressway
- Motorcycles were banned on the Padma Bridge "until further notice" in 2022 (UNB: https://unb.com.bd/category/Bangladesh/motorcycles-banned-on-padma-bridge-from-monday/95736). 2022 Dhaka-Mawa-Bhanga expressway motorcycle toll Tk30 and RHD recommended banning bikes on expressway (Dhaka Tribune: https://www.dhakatribune.com/bangladesh/290125/toll-collection-starts-on-dhaka-mawa-bhanga).
- 27 May 2026 Dhaka Tribune reports separate motorcycle lanes at the bridge and expressway, implying bikes are now allowed (https://www.dhakatribune.com/bangladesh/411213/padma-bridge-toll-collection-nears-tk10-crore-in). Current ban/allow status and toll: CONFLICT/UNVERIFIED. A third-party guide claims Tk100 per crossing (https://beta.tollguru.mapup.ai/bangladesh-toll), unverified. Check Bangladesh Bridge Authority before publishing.

## Second pass (accessed 2026-10-13)
- Padma Bridge: motorcycles allowed from 20 Apr 2023 (6:00 am start) after the 26 Jun 2022 ban; conditions: designated motorcycle booth and lane, no lane change, max 60 km/h, no overtaking, helmet and gear, no stopping or photos, max two people, permission to be cancelled for violations (Prothom Alo, 19 Apr 2023: https://www.prothomalo.com/bangladesh/6ic3qxg1hv). 23 Apr 2023: 23 riders fined Tk71,000 (Tk3,000 each mostly); 5,569 bikes crossed 6am-2pm (Bangla Daily Star: https://bangla.thedailystar.net/news/bangladesh/news-472621). May 2026: three separate motorcycle lanes at Mawa toll plaza (see main section). So the earlier "banned" status is superseded; a later re-ban is NOT FOUND. A result mentioned a rule that bikes are stopped at times; unverified.
- Toll: motorcycle Tk100 per gazette of 17 May 2022 (Bangla search snippet citing Bridge Division notification; Dhaka Tribune toll chart https://www.dhakatribune.com/bangladesh/270497/government-declares-padma-bridge-toll-chart not opened). Whether Tk100 is current: UNVERIFIED (padmabridge.gov.bd did not resolve). The Tk30 figure earlier is the Dhaka-Mawa expressway toll in 2022, a different segment.
