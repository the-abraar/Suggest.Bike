# Duty, tax and policy (Bangladesh motorcycles)

Access date for all citations: 2026-10-13. Status key: CONFIRMED (read in fetched article), PARTIAL, NOT FOUND. Search was via a summarising search tool, so no NBR/BRTA primary tables were reached; every rate below is secondhand news reporting.

## Import rules by cc (current + proposed)
- Motorcycles up to 165cc: no import restriction; may come as CBU or be assembled from imported components. CBU above 165cc is currently prohibited. Machinery/parts may be imported for local production up to 500cc. (Prothom Alo, 7 Jun 2026: https://en.prothomalo.com/amp/story/business/c7d38h8nz8, accessed 2026-10-13)
- Proposed Import Policy Order 2026-2029: lift CBU restriction up to 375cc, keep above 375cc banned (security forces excepted with ministry approval). Article said it was in final stage and could be issued June 2026; **issuance not verified** by me. (same source)
- Oct 2023: Home Ministry allowed bikes up to 375cc to operate, only if domestically made. (same source)

## Duty/VAT/SD structure
- **No complete current duty table by cc for CBU/CKD/SKD was found** (NOT FOUND). Rate figures below are historical fragments only; do not use as current.
- 2019 SRO (18 Mar 2019): full waiver of 20% supplementary duty (SD) for "second-tier" CKD manufacturers, four-stroke CKD bikes above 50cc and below 250cc; total duties/taxes of 91.88% still applied to second-tier after waiver; assemblers (complete chassis + parts) did not get the waiver; VAT registration mandatory. (Financial Express: https://thefinancialexpress.com.bd/public/trade/nbr-waives-20pc-sd-on-second-tier-manufacturers-ckd-bikes-1553142892, 2026-10-13) Older than 7 years, likely superseded.
- Motorcycle Industry Development Policy 2018: CKD import duty cut by 25 percentage points to 20% (as reported). (Daily Star Long Ride: https://www.thedailystar.net/supplements/long-ride/news/how-bangladesh-became-motorcycle-nation-4068976, 2026-10-13) Whether the 20% is the base duty or a total tax incidence is unclear. UNCERTAIN.
- 2015-16 report (via search summary): SD increase took total tax incidence for CKD to 130%, same as CBU. Historical only. (https://www.thedailystar.net/node/102424, 2026-10-13; not opened in full)
- FY2024-25 budget proposal: 10% import duty on parts for motorcycles above 250cc; duty on many motorcycle parts and related products 5% to 15%. Proposal, enactment not verified. (Dhaka Tribune 6 Jun 2024: https://www.dhakatribune.com/bangladesh/government-affairs/348622/motorcycles-over-250cc-price-may-go-up-in-fy25, 2026-10-13)
- FY2025-26 budget: broad tariff cuts reported (65 products' duty cut, 9 SD removed) but motorcycles not confirmed named. NOT FOUND for motorcycles. (search summary of https://www.dhakatribune.com/bangladesh/382918/import-duty-withdrawn-on-110-products)
- FY2026-27 budget: no motorcycle-specific duty change found. Cars 1,200-1,600cc total tax proposed up from 132.36% to ~156%. (Financial Express: https://thefinancialexpress.com.bd/economy/bangladesh/budget-2026-27-which-items-will-cost-more, via search summary, 2026-10-13)

## Advance income tax (AIT) on motorcycles, FY2026-27
- NBR drafted annual AIT: up to 110cc exempt; 111-125cc Tk2,000; 126-165cc Tk5,000; >165cc Tk10,000; later cut to Tk1,000/3,000/5,000; finally dropped entirely. Replacement: owners of bikes above 165cc must show income tax return proof (PSR) to register/renew. Source article dated 6 Jun 2026. BRTA data: 4.87 million registered motorcycles. (Dhaka Tribune: https://www.dhakatribune.com/amp/business/411857/nbr-drops-direct-tax-on-autorickshaws, 2026-10-13)
- Whether this survived the passed Finance Act: NOT VERIFIED.

## Local assembly / industry policy
- Seven full manufacturing plants: Honda, Suzuki, Yamaha, Bajaj, TVS, Hero, Runner. About 99% of bikes on road locally assembled/made (industry estimate). Hero plant 125,000 units/yr, 22 local components. (Daily Star Long Ride, link above)
- BMAMA: ~10 plants since 2018, ~Tk 80 billion investment; 2018 policy targeted 1 million bikes/yr by 2027; annual sales under 500,000. Under-invoicing by non-official importers alleged. (Prothom Alo link above)
- Sales: ~270k (2016), 387k (2017), 480k (2018); Jan-Jun 2025 257,632 units (+27% y/y). (Daily Star Long Ride)
- Market shifting from 100cc to 125-160cc priced Tk 2.2-4.5 lakh. (Daily Star Long Ride)

- FY2024-25 budget (Bangla Daily Star-affiliated report via itvbd, proposal of 6 Jun 2024): for engine CKD parts supplied to motorcycle makers the extra 3% additional import duty, regulatory duty and SD were exempted; 10% import duty proposed on parts for >250cc bikes; related products' duty 5% to 15%. No VAT or CBU figures given. (https://www.itvbd.com/economy/151765/বিদেশি-মোটরসাইকেলে-খরচ-বাড়ছে-দেশিতে-দাম-কমবে, fetched 2026-10-13)
- 2016-17: CKD kit duty cut 25 points to 20%; 99% of bikes now local (was ~95% imports 7 years earlier); 2023 sales fell 28% to 461,805 (ASI Motors); Bajaj ~30% share; of 10 factories 3 closed at reporting. (Bangla Daily Star, 19 Oct 2024: https://bangla.thedailystar.net/economy/news-623076)
- 2022-23 budget (Bangla Daily Star search snippet, not opened): no SD on imported >250cc bikes then. UNVERIFIED.
- FY2025-26 and FY2026-27 motorcycle-specific duty/SD/VAT: still NOT FOUND after Bangla and English searches (Daily Star, Financial Express, TBS Bangla results contained no motorcycle line). Treat as unknown; likely no major motorcycle change since none was reported, but that is inference, not evidence.
- E-bike manufacturing relief: S.R.O. 167-Law/2026/22/Customs (announced 8 Jun 2026, in force 11 Jun 2026, to 30 Jun 2030) exempts regulatory duty, SD and 15% VAT on machinery/inputs for nominated e-bike component vendors (Global Trade Alert: https://globaltradealert.org/state-act/100369-bangladesh-tariff-and-tax-exemptions-for-e-bike-and-component-manufacturing).
- Road tax paid at registration: Tk2,300 for 2 years or Tk11,500 for 10 years (Dhaka Tribune/Bangla report 12 May 2026; reproduced via TBS Bangla https://www.tbsnews.net/bangla/node/489596). Reconciles with registration Tk9,291/11,764 bundle only loosely; UNCERTAIN how components split.
- Registration fee components 2021: registration 2,000 (<=100cc) / 3,000 (>100cc), VAT on registration was 630/840 at the old fee (Prothom Alo 2 Feb 2021: https://www.prothomalo.com/business/industry/মোটরসাইকেলের-নিবন্ধন-মাশুল-অর্ধেক-কমছে).
- AIT collection mechanics: proposal was to tie to TIN or collect with tax token (khoborsangjog 19 May 2026: https://www.khoborsangjog.com/trade-and-business/112640/). Final outcome per Dhaka Tribune 6 Jun 2026: dropped, replaced by return-proof rule above 165cc. Whether the passed Finance Act matches: NOT VERIFIED.
- BRTA notice 1 Sep 2026: all vehicle papers (fitness, tax token, route permit) and licences renewable without fines if principal tax/fees paid by 29 Oct 2026; ownership transfer under Section 21 of Road Transport Act 2018, online at bsp.brta.gov.bd with biometric verification of seller, same day; licence 12 points (Sec 11(1)) (https://www.dhakatimes24.com/2026/09/01/411836).
