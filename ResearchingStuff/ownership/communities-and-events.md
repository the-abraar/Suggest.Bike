## Ride-sharing income
- Full-time Tk30,000-50,000/month, part-time Tk12,000-15,000; earnings fell as rider numbers rose; Pathao CEO claims commission can drop to 1% (company claim) (Daily Star 8 Nov 2024: https://www.thedailystar.net/business/news/inside-the-lives-ride-sharing-drivers-3748116)
- 2018: 20% fare share, part-time Tk20-30k, full-time Tk40-60k (Financial Express: https://old.thefinancialexpress.com.bd/trade/ride-sharing-creates-jobs-for-youths-1533354985) historical.
- 2018 PRI: ride-sharing ~Tk22 billion (Daily Star Long Ride link above).
