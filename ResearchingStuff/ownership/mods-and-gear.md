# Mods (legal status) and riding gear

Access date for all citations: 2026-10-13.

## Modification law
- Road Transport Act 2018 bars changing BRTA-recorded specs (dimensions, seats, wheels, brakes, gears, horn, lights, reflectors, exhaust system, emissions, sound level) without BRTA permission; like-for-like part replacement matching registered specs needs no permission. (TBS, 23 Nov 2019: https://www.tbsnews.net/node/25367)
- Penalty CONFLICT: that article says 1-3 years jail and/or up to Tk3 lakh fine; another TBS piece lists a Tk3 lakh fine for unlawful modification (https://www.tbsnews.net/node/21549). Act text not checked; UNCERTAIN whether jail applies.
- No registration/number plate: Tk50,000 fine or six months jail or both (TBS node/21549, via search summary).
- ECU remaps, aftermarket exhaust legality specifics, altered plates: NOT FOUND. Treat aftermarket exhaust and engine mods as requiring BRTA permission per the Act.
