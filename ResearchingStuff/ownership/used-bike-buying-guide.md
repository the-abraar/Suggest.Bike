# Used-bike buying guide

Access date for all citations: 2026-10-13. Evidence is thin; most checklists below are general and marked as such.

## Paperwork and fraud
- Brokers outside BRTA collude on name transfers without owner present, claiming owner is old/sick; both buyer and seller should be present at BRTA. Source is a Bikroy seller ad, not an authority: UNCERTAIN. (https://bikroy.com/uttara/motorcycles-and-scooters/honda-unicorn-2010-P9RuHDcaKAGRZem3k5Tvhau.html)
- From 15 Sep 2022 registration authority must verify a driving licence when issuing a registration number. (TBS: https://www.tbsnews.net/bangladesh/no-bike-registration-without-driving-licence-brta-453694)
- Owners of bikes above 165cc must show tax return proof to register/renew from FY2026-27 (see duty file; verify enactment). (https://www.dhakatribune.com/amp/business/411857/nbr-drops-direct-tax-on-autorickshaws)
- Lost-document process (useful when checking a seller's claims): RC duplicate needs police GD, traffic clearance, forms, 3 photos, deposit slip; tax token duplicate free. (BikeBD: https://bikebd.com/blog/motorcycle-document-lost-bangladesh)
- General (non-BD) advice from a BD buying guide: mechanic inspection, dealer purchase gives warranty/guarantee at slightly higher price. (https://steemit.com/tech/@frank2sataine/things-to-know-before-buying-a-new-motorcycle) low-quality source.
- Official BRTA digital registration check / stolen-vehicle check: NOT FOUND. Match engine/chassis numbers to RC (general practice, not BD-sourced).

## Illegal Indian bikes
- Beanibazar (Sylhet) reported as thriving on illegal Indian bikes (Financial Express: https://thefinancialexpress.com.bd/national/beanibazar-thrives-on-illegal-indian-bikes-1568202317; article older, not read in full). Avoid unregistered/smuggled bikes: cannot be registered.

## Second pass (accessed 2026-10-13)
- Ownership transfer: Road Transport Act 2018 Section 21 requires seller to transfer after sale; if not, previous owner may remain liable for taxes; BRTA says transfer is online at bsp.brta.gov.bd with seller's biometric verification, same-day possible (BRTA notice via https://www.dhakatimes24.com/2026/09/01/411836, notice date 1 Sep 2026). This makes the seller-present rule official; broker "owner is sick" claims should be refused.
- BRTA Service Portal (bsp.brta.gov.bd) is open 8:00-22:00 BST; I could not confirm a public stolen/registration lookup there (https://bsp.brta.gov.bd). Still NOT FOUND.
- BRTA service list incl. duplicate and digital registration certificate (Kushtia BRTA page, old: https://brta.kushtia.gov.bd/en/site/page/jPuU-সেবার-তালিকা). Smart-card RC and RFID plates mandatory for motorcycles (BikeBD lost-document guide).
