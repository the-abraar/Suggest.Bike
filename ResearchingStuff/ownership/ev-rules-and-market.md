# EV rules and market (two-wheelers)

Access date for all citations: 2026-10-13.

## Rules
- E-bikes with pedal option are waived from BRTA registration; Walton Takyon Leo (Tk49,850-59,850 by battery) needs no registration; Takyon 1.00 (Tk127,750) was first BRTA-approved e-bike. (TBS: https://www.tbsnews.net/economy/corporates/walton-launches-affordable-e-bike-584682; Walton: https://www.waltondigitech.com/news/countrys-first-brta-approved-walton-e-bike-hits-market-km-cost-10-15-paisa) Prices are from 2021-22; stale.
- Runner eWave Electrica Tk125,000, discontinued, BRTA registration required (https://bikebd.com/ev/runner-ewave-electrica).
- 2023 "Policy on Registration and Movement of Electric Motor Vehicles" exists; details not read (search summary of https://online91.thedailystar.net/business/economy/news/govt-readies-rules-pave-way-electric-vehicles-2912346).
- Draft Electric Three-Wheeler Management Policy 2025 (three-wheelers only) (Dhaka Tribune: https://www.dhakatribune.com/bangladesh/388489/tougher-policy-soon-to-regulate-battery-run).

## Duty / incentives
- FY2026-27 draft: EVs under $25,000 duty 93% to 66-80%; ICE total tax up; VAT on locally assembled EVs 5% to 2030; charger duties removed. No two-wheeler-specific rates given. (electrive, 24 Jun 2026: https://www.electrive.com/es/2026/06/24/se-dan-a-conocer-incentivos-para-los-vehiculos-electricos-en-banglades-mientras-byd-se-plantea-su-expansion/)
- Runner sells Yadea two-wheelers (same source). Charging: 32 public stations, ~250 home chargers (same source).
- Electric bike duty by HS code, final budget text, brand price list: NOT FOUND.
