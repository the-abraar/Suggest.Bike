# Yamaha R15 / MT-15 variant decoder
Access 2026-10-13. Source: Wikipedia https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yamaha_YZF-R15 (page itself flagged inconsistent; check primary sources).

- V1 (2008): 149.5cc liquid-cooled 4-valve SOHC, 16.4 hp@8500, 14.5 Nm, 6-speed, 270mm front/220mm rear disc, tyres 80/90-17 & 100/80-17, 12 L tank, no ABS mentioned.
- V2.0 (India Sept 2011): revised ECU, swingarm, split seat, LED taillight, wider tyres (front 90/80-17, rear 130/70-17), no VVA; power given as 16.4 hp in specs but "about 17 hp" in text (conflict); ~136 kg (unreferenced). R15 v2 "never came with" ABS. Variants R15S, M-Slaz/Xabre/TFX 150 (unfaired).
- V3.0 (Indonesia Jan 2017): 155 cc (155.7 infobox vs 155.1 text - conflict), VVA, 19.0 hp@10000, 14.7 Nm, assist+slipper clutch, USD fork (except India), 282mm disc, tyres 100/80-17 & 140/70-17, 11 L tank, all-LED, LCD. MT-15 naked (conventional fork in India), XSR 155.
- V4.0 (India 21 Sept 2021): R15M flagship, traction control, quickshifter, Deltabox frame. No engine/ABS figures on that page.
- BD-specific: UNVERIFIED (not researched: BD import route, grey imports, ABS spec in BD, engine prefix, faults, recalls, resale).
