# Honda Hornet 160R / X-Blade decoder (BD)
Access 2026-10-13. Source: https://www.bikebd.com/blog/honda-cb-hornet-160r-vs-honda-xblade-feature-comparison

- Both: 162.71cc air-cooled 2-valve SOHC, carb, CDI, 5-speed, 12 L tank, diamond frame.
- Hornet 160R: 138 kg STD / 140 kg CBS; tyres 100/80-17 & 140/70-17; 15.3 PS@8500, 14.76 Nm@6500; 276mm front, 220mm rear disc or 130mm drum (as listed); 10-spoke alloy; BDT 1,89,800 / 2,01,800 CBS (dated). Ground clearance 164 mm.
- X-Blade: 141 kg; tyres 80/100-17 & 130/70-17; text says 14.13 PS (possible typo), 13.9 Nm; front disc, rear 130mm drum; 5-spoke; BDT 1,72,900.
- Hornet launch release: 163cc, 15.3 ps, 14.76 Nm, warranty 2 years/20,000 km: https://bdhonda.com/media-center/pr-details/41
- ABS variant older post: 2,55,000 BDT (dated) via search summary.
- Conflicting X-Blade BD launch date (Dec 2019 vs India Dec 2018): see https://www.bikebd.com/price/honda-x-blade-160-price-in-bd
- CB Shine / SP125 / Livo: no BD-specific data found - UNVERIFIED. Assembly, faults, recalls, resale: not found.
