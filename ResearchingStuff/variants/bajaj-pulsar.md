- NS160: 2017; 15.5 PS, 14.6 Nm; launch had narrower rear tyre/no rear disc, later 120/80 + disc; 2024 LED + LCD.
- NS200: Apr 2012, 200cc liquid-cooled 4-valve triple-spark; no FI; power 23.17 bhp vs 24.5 PS (conflict); reintroduced 2017 BS4, ABS variant later 2017.
- N250: 2021, 24.2 PS, 21.5 Nm.
