# Yamaha FZ / FZS variant decoder (BD)
Access date for all citations: 2026-10-13. Status: second pass. Items without a source are listed under "Unverified".

## Generations (verified)
- **FZ V1 (carb) -> FZ FI V2 (India spec changes):** 153cc -> 149cc (both air-cooled 2-valve SOHC); carb -> FI; 14 PS@7500 -> 13.1 PS@8000; 13.6 -> 12.8 Nm; 135 -> 132 kg; wheelbase 1334 -> 1330 mm; tank 12 L unchanged; gear ratios unchanged; no kick starter on V2; battery 5Ah -> 4Ah; LCD console; pillion seat 10 mm wider. https://bikeadvice.in/new-fz-fi-v2-vs-old-fz-spec-comparison-changes/
- **Rear sprocket:** V1 40T, V2 41T; V2 BSIV-ready, same catalyser; V2 about 3 kg lighter, seat 5 mm lower, rear damping and tyre profile changed, braking hardware same. https://www.motorbeam.com/?p=117400
- **FZS FI V2 (BD, 2017):** launched as CBU at BDT 2,65,000; the BikeBD page says ACI later assembles it CKD (year unclear on page) at 2,45,000. 13 BHP@8000, 12.8 Nm@6000, 5-speed, 118 km/h solo (reviewer). Dual-disc "DD" variant adds 282 mm front + 220 mm rear disc, 10-spoke alloys, new mirror, matt colours only; engine/chassis/suspension unchanged vs single-disc V2. O-ring chain on the DD. https://bikebd.com/blog/yamaha-fzs-fi-v2-dual-disc-test-ride-review
- **Yamaha 150cc BD range:** Fazer Fi V2, FZS Fi V2 DD, FZS Fi V3; V2s India-made, discontinued in India, replaced by V3; BD discontinuation is only a rumour article. https://bikebd.com/blog/yamaha-discontinue-fazer-fzs-fi-v2-dd-bangladesh
- **FZS FI V3 (BD):** 13 BHP/12.8 Nm; about 5 kg heavier than V2; 13 L tank; LED headlight; front+rear disc with front single-channel ABS; sealed chain (V2 chain loosened periodically); key moved to tank; CBU, 153% tax, price 295,000 BDT (dated). https://bikebd.com/blog/yamaha-fzs-fi-v3-review-team-bikebd
- **FZS V4 (BD):** launch event 28 Sept 2024, ICCB Dhaka; 150cc FI, Class D headlights with position lamps, LCD meter, LED flashers, Yamaha Motorcycle Connect (Bluetooth), single-channel ABS + traction control, 6 colours, Tk 299,500. Page does not say CKD or CBU, nor give power/weight. https://www.aci-bd.com/events/aci-motors-launches-yamaha-fzs-version-4.0-in-bangladesh.html (re-verified by full fetch). "Same chassis as V3" claim: still only a search-summary paraphrase of https://www.bikebd.com/bike-compare/yamaha-fzs-v4/vs/bajaj-pulsar-180f - UNVERIFIED.

## Throttle cable callout
- **FZ FI V2: dual throttle cables** per Motorbeam ("the FZ FI now getting dual throttle cables"; carb V1 has a softer throttle). Single source, Indian V1-vs-V2 FI test: https://www.motorbeam.com/?p=117400
- **Carb FZ-16/FZ-S (old):** owner forum says early units had double cable and Yamaha later fitted a single cable over a dual-cable carb; dual-cable carb works with single cable; changing cable type needs the right switch console changed. Anecdotal: https://www.xbhp.com/talkies/motorcycle-ownership-experiences/1281-yamaha-fz-16-fz-s?p=1580503
- These two sources look inconsistent on which version has which; the exact cutover year/version is UNVERIFIED. Bangla search found nothing further.

## Known faults / owner folk wisdom (BikeBD owner reviews, not Yamaha data)
- V2 35,000 km owner (Khulna purchase Tk 2,55,000): weak headlight, low acceleration, tappet adjustment holds briefly, underbelly rubs with pillion over speed breakers; replaced clutch cable x3, accelerator cable x3, air filter x3, chain sprocket x1; 4 free + 3 paid services. https://bikebd.com/blog/yamaha-fzs-fi-v2-35000-km-user-review
- V2 reviewers: big turning radius, FI needs frequent cleaning/premium fuel (RON 92) at authorised centres rare outside Dhaka, 2017 CBU chain noisy. https://bikebd.com/blog/yamaha-fzs-fi-v2-dual-disc-test-ride-review
- V3: engine sensitive to bad fuel; vibration near 6,500 rpm; 4 kg+ weight gain. (V3 review above)
- Other owner reviews to read: https://www.bikebd.com/blog/yamaha-fzs-fi-v2-20000-km-user-review-sumon , https://bikebd.com/blog/10200km-riding-experience-yamaha-fzs-fi-v3 (not fully read).
