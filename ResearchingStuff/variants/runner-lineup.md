# Runner lineup (BD)
Access date for all citations: 2026-10-13.
- Company: Runner Automobiles, founded 2000 as importer, public ltd Jan 2012, factory Bhaluka, Mymensingh; 2016 agreement with UM Motorcycles (UM-Runner bikes from Q2 2018); past partners Dayang, Lohia; Bajaj for three-wheelers (Wikipedia, weakly sourced): https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Runner_Automobiles
- Bullet 100 (BikeBD): made/assembled in Bangladesh; 100.53cc carb, 6.43 BHP@7500, 7 Nm@5500, 8.8:1, 121.5 kg, single disc, tube tyres, kick+electric, Tk 106,000; pros/cons listed incl. vibration, low-power headlight, brakes. A different BikeBD URL labels it "runner-dayang-bullet-100" (Dayang lineage suggested, unconfirmed). https://bikebd.com/price/runner-dayang-bullet-100
- Turbo 125 V2 Matt (BikeBD compare): single-cyl air-cooled SOHC, 11.25 power / 11 torque (units unstated), 5-speed, wet multiplate clutch, carb, 132,000-135,000 BDT. https://www.bikebd.com/bike-compare/runner-turbo-v2/vs/hero-splendor-plus
- Knight Rider V2: described as 150cc, LED headlight, digital cluster, discs both ends - from search summary only, UNVERIFIED. Bikroy ads exist for 2017-2020 units: https://bikroy.com/bn/motorcycles-and-scooters/runner-knight-rider-v2-2020
- Engine origin of Turbo/Knight Rider, generation differences, faults, recalls, resale: not found.
