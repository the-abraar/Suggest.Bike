# Decoding engine / chassis numbers
Access date for all citations: 2026-10-13.
## Verified: VIN structure and Indian WMIs
- Chassis number = 17-char VIN: positions 1-3 WMI, 4-9 descriptor (maker-specific), 10-17 identifier; position 10 is model year in North America, usually 0 elsewhere; letters I, O, Q never used. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_identification_number
- Indian two-wheeler WMIs (Wikipedia list "selection"): MBL Hero, MD2 Bajaj, MD6 TVS, ME4 Honda Motorcycle & Scooter India, ME1 India Yamaha Motor (ME1 via fetched Wikipedia answer; also Scribd older WMI list), MBF/ME3 Royal Enfield. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_identification_number ; https://www.carcheckervin.com/vin-country-codes/india
- So a bike whose chassis starts ME1 is India-Yamaha-made, MD2 Bajaj, etc. Position 4+ per-model codes and year letters: NOT found; do not assume the North American year table applies to Indian bikes.
