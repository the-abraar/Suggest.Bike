## Importer / assembler
- TVS Auto Bangladesh Ltd (TVSABL): joint venture of T V Sundram Iyengar & Sons (India) and Rian Motors Ltd (now REL Motors, sister of Rangs Electronics, est. 1984) [S1][S2]. Daily Star 2007 reported the JV would assemble and market TVS locally; the line-up then included the Victor, a 100cc "StaR" range, an electric-start GLX and Scooty [S3]. BikeBD says operations began 2007 (also "July 2007") [S1][S2]; became "second-largest two-wheeler seller" within seven years (BikeBD claim) [S2].
- Assembly: Atlas Bangladesh Ltd plant, Tongi, Gazipur, under a Feb 2019 agreement [S1]. 
- CONFLICT: a BikeBD headline names PRAN-RFL as a new TVS distributor while the page FAQ still names TVSABL [S1]; not resolved.
- A model called "Flame" was launched in BD (date not given) [search summary of S2].

## Dated BD launches (TVS press releases fetched in full)
- **Apache RTR 160 4V, BD launch 12 Nov 2018**: **carburettor** variant, **Double Disc** variant for BD; 159.7cc, 4-valve, oil-cooled, **16.5 PS @8000, 14.8 Nm @6500**, 5-speed, 113 km/h; colours RR Red, Racing Black, Racing Blue; no price [S7].
- **8 Apr 2019 release (4 products)**: Apache RTR 160 4V **single disc** (carb, same specs), TVS Max 125 (123.53cc), Metro Special Edition 100cc (KLS/ELS), XL100 Heavy Duty i-Touch Start (99.7cc) [S8].
- **2020 BikeBD launch article** (digital event by TVSABL MD/CEO; date inferred 2020 from URL): carb 4V Single Disc 186,900, Dual Disc 196,900, no ABS yet; 16.3 BHP/14.8 Nm, 149 kg, Showa mono, LED headlamp, SmartXonnect Bluetooth cluster [S9]. (16.3 vs 16.5 PS = CONFLICT between BikeBD and TVS.)
- **Apache RTR 160 2V ABS, BD launch 22 Sep 2022**: 159.7cc 2-valve SOHC **air-cooled, fuel-injected**, 14.14 PS@8000, 13.03 Nm@6750, single-channel ABS [S10]. So in BD: carb 4V (2018-) preceded the FI 2V (2022).
- **Apache RTR 160 4V Fi (BD)**: "recent addition", BS6, EFI replaces carb, single-channel ABS, riding modes sport/urban/rain, 17.30 BHP@9250, 14.8 Nm, 146 kg, Tk 274,900; **launch date not given** [S11]. The carb-to-FI 4V changeover year in BD is therefore **still UNVERIFIED** (between 2020 and 2026).
- **TVS Raider 125 BD launch 7 Mar 2022**: 124.8cc air-oil-cooled 3V, 12.9 PS@8000, 5-speed, LCD speedo, i-Touch Start; colours Red, Blue, Black, Yellow [S12]. A BikeBD article (Bashundhara ICCB event, a later relaunch/new version; date unclear) gives 144,900 BDT [S12b].
