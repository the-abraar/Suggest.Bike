# Hero / Hero Honda in Bangladesh

Convention: `[Sn]` = Sources below, accessed 2026-10-13. Flags VERIFIED / CONFLICT / UNVERIFIED. BikeBD date stamps unreliable [S1]. Nothing invented.

## Importer / assembler
- Hero MotoCorp BD operations began 21 April 2014 as a JV of Nitol-Niloy Motors and Hero MotoCorp; "Niloy Motors Limited is the manufacturer and official distributor"; plant at Naopara, Jessore described as Hero's first overseas plant; 300+ dealers (BikeBD claims) [S1].
- Hero Honda corporate: 1984-2011 JV, Honda stake bought out 2011, renamed Hero [S1]; slide-deck sources say Hunk 2007, Splendor 1994, CBZ Xtreme 2006 [S2]. Who imported Hero Honda bikes in BD before 2014: **not found**.
- Hero Honda CBZ Xtreme: launched 2006, discontinued 2012, 149.2cc, 14.4 BHP@8500, 12.8 Nm [S3].
- Hero Honda Hunk (BD): discontinued; 149.2cc air-cooled, 14 BHP@8500, 12.8 Nm, carburettor, kick+electric, ~40 km/l claimed, 146 kg, last listed price Tk 187,500 (page also shows "0") — CONFLICT/ unreliable; years sold not given [S4]. Hero Honda Glamour (125cc, drum): discontinued [S5].
