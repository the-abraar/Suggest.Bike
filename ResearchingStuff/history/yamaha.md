# Yamaha in Bangladesh

Access date for all citations: 2026-10-13. Status: PARTIAL. Only what was verified is recorded; gaps are listed explicitly. BikeBD pages carry spurious "Last updated 12-Oct-2026" stamps, so their dates are unreliable and only body text is used.

## Importer and entry
- Yamaha entered Bangladesh in 2016 through ACI Motors Ltd (subsidiary of ACI Group), sole distributor/technical partner. [Daily Star 8 yrs, 17 Nov 2024](https://d11.thedailystar.net/business/organisation-news/news/yamaha-celebrates-8-years-aci-motors-3755041); [Daily Star 7 yrs summary via search, TBS](https://www.tbsnews.net/node/737974)
- ACI Motors imported Yamaha as CBU (fully built, from India) at least through 2018; the 2018 FZS Fi V2 Dual Disc article attributes its high price to CBU import. [BikeBD ACI FZS V2 DD](https://www.bikebd.com/blog/aci-motors-launch-yamaha-fzs-fi-v2-bangladesh)
- 99+ dealers (2022) / 116+ 3S points (2023) per press reports. [TBS](https://www.tbsnews.net/node/737974)
- ACI claims FZS V3 was the first ABS-equipped bike in Bangladesh and introduced the first "Fi servicing tool". [ACI 4 years](https://aci-bd.com/all-news/yamaha-bangladesh-celebrates-4-years-journey-with-aci-motors.html)
- UNVERIFIED/OPEN: pre-2016 Yamaha presence (RX100, RX135, YBR125, Libero etc.). No Bangladesh source found stating import years or importer for these. BikeBD's "Legends of Yamaha" lists RX100 (98cc 2-stroke; 11 hp; produced 1985-1996 in India) but gives no Bangladesh dates. [BikeBD legends](https://www.bikeBD.com/blog/yamaha-legends-of-yamaha-campaign) Pre-2016 FZ-S V1 launch in BD is claimed "2008" on that page (this is the Indian launch year; BD year unverified).

### FZS V2 niche questions (user-requested)
- Carb with 1 vs 2 throttle cables, ECU/tank/graphics differences, BD-assembled vs India-made, 2013-2015 differences: NOT FOUND in any source. Two searches returned nothing. - BD FZS V2 Dual Disc (2018): 149cc FI, 13 BHP, 12.8 Nm, 5-speed, only colour Armada Blue, 10-spoke alloy wheels, new mirrors; booking opened 1 May 2018, 30,000 BDT deposit, stated price 265,000 BDT (expected 260-270k). Article itself contradicts: calls "dual disc" but lists a "220mm rear disc". Treat as conflicting. [BikeBD](https://www.bikebd.com/blog/aci-motors-launch-yamaha-fzs-fi-v2-bangladesh)
- BD-reported owner numbers (BikeBD test, 2017): 38 km/l city, 42-45 highway, top speed 120 km/h. [same]
- Fazer FI V2 (BD): 149cc, 13.1 PS @8000, 12.8 Nm @6000, BS4, 137 kg, front 267mm disc / rear 130mm drum, colours Burning Red, Midnight Black, Marble White; price 275,000 BDT at time of article (date unstated). Article says FI is less powerful than carb Fazer, and that "Blue Core" was added to fix mileage complaints of the earlier Fazer/FZS. [BikeBD](https://www.bikebd.com/blog/yamaha-fazer-fi-version-2)
- FZS V2 spec card: bore 57.3 x stroke 57.9, CR 9.5:1, 132 kg wet, electric start only. [BikeBD](https://testserver.bikebd.com/price/yamaha-fzs-v2/overview)

- BS3/BS4/BS6: BD has "FZS V3 ABS BS4" and "FZS V3 BS6" labels on BikeBD. Fazer FI V2 listed BS4. [BikeBD Yamaha](https://www.bikebd.com/brand/yamaha) BD does not legally enforce Bharat Stage; labels reflect India-origin units (interpretation, unverified).

## Corrections and additions to ACI / assembly history
- ACI Motors began Yamaha sales 2016 (mid-Sept sales start per 2016 report); CKD plant at Borochala, Sreepur, Gazipur, about 6 acres, ~Tk 100 crore, target 60,000 bikes/yr, inaugurated by video conference (ACI page says 11 May 2019; Daily Star says ceremony 10 May 2019, minor conflict). [ACI](https://www.aci-bd.com/all-news/aci-motors-inaugurated-yamaha-ckd-factory.html), [Daily Star](https://www.thedailystar.net/business/news/yamaha-bikes-be-built-here-next-year-1742338)
- FZS V3 / FZ V3 launched in BD 28 Apr 2019 at Le Meridien Dhaka: FZS FI V3 Tk 295,000 (3 colours), FZ V3 Tk 290,000 (2 colours); pre-booking from 10 Apr, 1,000 units booked; ACI blamed 152% import duty on 150cc+; plant said to bring locally assembled bikes "by next July" (2019). Before then, imports were CBU. [BikeBD / Daily Star-style report](https://www.thedailystar.net/business/aci-brings-new-yamaha-bikes-in-bangladesh-1737193)
- ACI's early lineup article: R15 V2.0, R15S, Fazer-FI V2.0, FZS-FI V2.0 (different front panel/headlight from Fazer-FI), SZ-RR V2.0 (carburetted, new body/graphics), Saluto 125 (disc/drum and drum/drum). [BikeBD](https://www.bikebd.com/blog/aci-motors-started-delivery-yamaha-motorcycle-in-bangladesh) Whether the CBU units were from India: article cites Yamaha Motor India for specs only.
- Saluto Armada Blue launched 5 Mar 2020 (Tk 129,000, 8.1 bhp, 10.1 Nm, engine unchanged); Ray ZR Street Rally 125 launched Nov 2021 (five-year anniversary). [ACI](https://aci-bd.com/all-news/launching-of-yamaha-saluto-armada-blue.html), [BikeBD](https://www.bikebd.com/blog/yamaha-saluto-armada-blue-launched-bangladesh), [Financial Express](https://mail.thefinancialexpress.com.bd/trade/yamaha-launches-new-scooter-street-rally-125cc-in-bangladesh-1636964823)

## RX100 / RX135 / RXS115 / Libero / YBR125
- RX100 (India, Escorts): CKD-assembled in India Nov 1985 to Mar 1996; 98cc 2-stroke, 11 PS, 4-speed; 6V to 12V change; speedometers Yenkay ("RAJDOOT") then Pricol; three decal types; RXG (132cc) then RX135 (rebadged RXG); 14 bhp 5-speed RX135/RXZ in 2000-01; short-lived 4-speed RX135 in 2003. Source is a 2008 forum post copied from Wikipedia; treat as lore. [xBhp](https://www.xbhp.com/talkies/general-biking-discussion/1759-yamaha-rx-100-history)
- In BD: BikeBD says RX100 adopted by all classes, red popular, praised electrics/"Made in Japan" badge; criticised noise, weak headlight, tyres. No importer or BD years given. [BikeBD RX100](https://www.bikebd.com/blog/yamaha-rx-100) Bikroy shows used 1994-1998 units at roughly Tk 20,000-90,000 (search snippet). [Bikroy](https://bikroy.com/en/ads/bangladesh/motorbikes-scooters/yamaha/rx100) Whether BD units were Japanese-built vs Indian is UNVERIFIED.
- RXS115 (2-stroke 115cc, ~16 HP, 5-speed, front disc) listed among older models in BD; no year/importer. [BikeBD old models](https://www.bikebd.com/bn/blog/%E0%A6%AC%E0%A6%BE%E0%A6%82%E0%A6%B2%E0%A6%BE%E0%A6%A6%E0%A7%87%E0%A6%B6%E0%A7%87%E0%A6%B0-%E0%A6%B8%E0%A6%AC-%E0%A6%A5%E0%A7%87%E0%A6%95%E0%A7%87-%E0%A7%AB%E0%A6%9F%E0%A6%BF-%E0%A6%AA%E0%A7%81)
- RX135, Libero, YBR125 in BD: STILL NO SOURCE FOUND (searched English and Bangla). Prothom Alo and Jugantor history pieces had no BD brand detail (one 403).
