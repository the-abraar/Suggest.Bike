# Electric two-wheelers in BD

Access date for all citations: 2026-10-13. First pass; thin.

## Walton Takyon
- Walton Digi-Tech Industries Ltd sells e-bikes under the Takyon brand and is described as having launched BD's first electric bike. [Bangladesh Monitor](https://bangladeshmonitor.com.bd/en/walton-makes-countrys-first-electric-bike) (launch date not retrieved)
- Takyon Leo (TBS, 13 Feb 2023): three variants 12 Ah Tk49,850 / 20 Ah Tk56,850 / 23 Ah Tk59,850; 40-80 km range; 6-8 h charge; has pedals so no BRTA registration needed (Walton says first in segment with this approval); Leo assembled locally, full manufacturing planned. Earlier Takyon 1.00 cost Tk127,750. [TBS](https://www.tbsnews.net/economy/corporates/walton-launches-affordable-e-bike-584682)
- Current Takyon listings (1.20, Fusion 25FZ etc., about 80,000-185,000 BDT) per BikeBD; indicative only. [BikeBD](https://bikebd.com/ev/walton-takyon)
- A 2023 MoU with Skoot for joint EV manufacturing was reported (search snippet; UNVERIFIED).
