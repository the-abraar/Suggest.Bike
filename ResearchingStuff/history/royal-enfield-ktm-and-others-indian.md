# Royal Enfield, KTM, Vespa/Piaggio and other Indian-origin brands in Bangladesh

Convention: `[Sn]` = Sources below, accessed 2026-10-13. Flags VERIFIED / CONFLICT / UNVERIFIED. Nothing invented.

## Royal Enfield
- Official entry Oct 2024 via IFAD Motors (IFAD Group): launch 21 Oct 2024 (pre-booking 22 Oct) of Hunter, Classic, Bullet, Meteor 350; prices Tk 3.4-4.35 lakh (Hunter 3.4, Classic 4.5, Bullet 4.1, Meteor 4.35 in one report); local assembly plant at Chauddagram, Cumilla, 8 acres, 30,000/yr capacity, everything but engine assembled locally; ~3,000 orders in 10 minutes; 13,000 sold in the first year, about half Classic 350 [S1][S2]. Enabled by government lifting the 165cc cap to 375cc in 2023 [S1]. (Dates differ by one day across reports.)
- Current list Sept 2026: Meteor 350 (435,000-508,000), Classic 350 variants (412,000-483,000), Bullet 350 417,500 (card shows 437,500 CONFLICT), Hunter 350 346,500-371,500; no discontinued models listed [S3].
- Pre-2024 grey-market Royal Enfield / Rajdoot Bullet in BD: **not found**.

## KTM
- Official distributor Runner Automobiles; BD cap then limited imports to 125cc; before that KTM came via grey importers; models Duke 125 and RC125 [S4]. Date not stated.
- Listed: RC 125 European 635,000 (disc.), RC 125 Indian ABS 566,000 (disc.), RC 125 (2017) 525,000 (disc.), RC 125 2022 (443,000 vs 370,000 CONFLICT), Duke 125 European 370,000, Duke 125 Indian 300,000 [S5]. Larger KTMs only "upcoming" [S5].
- Runner also launched BOLT 165 R in Aug 2020 (Tk 169,000, 165cc, first 165cc in BD) [S6].

## Vespa / Piaggio / Aprilia
- BikeBD: Vespas sold in BD from the 1980s through unofficial imports; stopped in late 1990s; official return via Runner Automobiles (displayed at Dhaka Motor Show 2019); another BikeBD article credits KR Industries as earlier distributor — timeline CONFLICT [S7][S8]. Uttara Motors (Bajaj) imported Vespa 150 from India earlier [S9].
- Models listed 2026: VXL 125/150, SXL 125/150, Primavera, Sprint, Elegante 150, LX 125, Notte 125; "Vespa 150" discontinued [S7]. Aprilia SR 150 mentioned [S7].

## Jawa/Yezdi, Rajdoot/Escorts, Kinetic, LML, Mahindra
No Bangladesh-specific source found for any of these (searches returned only Indian history: Ideal Jawa/Yezdi from 1960, Yezdi name from 1968, production stopped 1990 [S10]). Treat all BD claims as UNVERIFIED/absent.
