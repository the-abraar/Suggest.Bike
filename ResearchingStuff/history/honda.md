# Honda in Bangladesh

Access date for all citations: 2026-10-13. Status: PARTIAL. BikeBD "last updated" stamps are unreliable.

## Importer / assembler
- Bangladesh Honda Pvt Ltd (BHL): Honda Motor + Asian Honda Motor hold 70%, state-owned Bangladesh Steel & Engineering Corporation (BSEC) 30%. CKD assembly began 2013 at a rented Gazipur factory, first model CD80, 66 units in year one via three dealerships. Own factory opened 2018 in Abdul Monem Economic Zone, Munshiganj (about 49,000 bikes first year). Cumulative output: 100k (Jan 2019), 150k (Nov 2019), 200k (~22 Dec 2020). CBU duty 149% vs CKD 88%; 49-57% inside the economic zone. [Dhaka Tribune](https://www.dhakatribune.com/amp/business/commerce/234204/honda-s-signifies-remarkable-journey-in-bangladesh)
- A 2013 report says Honda was preparing assembly with BSEC. [Daily Star 2013](https://www.thedailystar.net/news/suzuki-teams-up-with-rancon-to-assemble-motorcycles)
- BHL started sales end of Dec 2013, local CD80 production from Oct 2013; Dream Neo units initially brought from Honda India (HMSI). (Search-summary of seair.co.in; secondary) [seair](https://www.seair.co.in/exim-news/Bangladesh-Honda-Private-Limited-Bhl-Imports-Neo-From-India.aspx)
- Dream 110 launched at Tk 89,900 (c. late 2020). [Dhaka Tribune](https://www.dhakatribune.com/amp/business/commerce/234204/honda-s-signifies-remarkable-journey-in-bangladesh)
- Pre-2013 Honda importers: NOT FOUND. No source names any importer for 1960s-2012. UNVERIFIED lore: "Honda" became a generic word for motorcycles in BD because of CD80. [BikeBD CD80](https://www.bikebd.com/price/honda-cd80)

## Historic models
- Honda H100 (1980), H100A (1982), H100S (1985); reached Bangladesh early 1986 (H100/H100A already on roads); 2-stroke single; "discontinued from 1992" per article (contradicted by its claim it ruled for three decades). Variants H100S2, H100S-J, H100SG, H100SD. Claimed 20 kmpl (unsourced). No importer stated. [BikeBD H100S](https://bikebd.com/blog/honda-h100s-cdi) Also names Cub 50, CD80, later CG125 as contemporaries (via search summary).
- CD80: 4-stroke OHC carburetor, kick start, 4-speed; spec card conflicts 72cc vs 80cc; 4.2 BHP @6500, 5.16 Nm, CR 8.8:1, ~82 kg; marked discontinued; sold earlier CBU and CKD (retailer claim) before BHL local assembly. [BikeBD CD80](https://www.bikebd.com/price/honda-cd80)
- CD70 / C70 / CG125 in BD: no Bangladesh importer, years or variants found. BikeBD pages carry zero prices and blank distributor fields. [BikeBD compare](https://www.bikebd.com/bike-compare/honda-c-70-1970/vs/honda-cd70); CG125 1997 page shows Not-Available. [BikeBD CG125](https://testserver.bikebd.com/price/honda-cg-125-1997)

## Launch dates (Bangladesh)
- CB Hornet 160R: launched at ICCB Dhaka, two-day event from evening of 16 Feb 2018; price Tk 199,800 (single disc), announced by BHL MD/CEO Yuichiro Ishii; 163cc, 15.1 BHP, 14.7 Nm, HET, AHO, colours Striking Green / Sports Red / Athletic Blue Metallic; 2-yr/20,000 km engine warranty. Price cut to Tk 189,800 Jan 2019 (search snippet); later list prices 169.8k (std), 212k (CBS), 255k (ABS). 2019 ABS version announced separately. [BikeBD launch](https://bikebd.com/blog/honda-cb-hornet-launching-program-2018), [BHL PR 41 title via search](https://bdhonda.com/media-center/pr-details/41)
- X-Blade 160: launched 13-14 December (2019 per search summary) at ICCB; STD Tk 172,900 (another source says 174,900: conflict); 160cc, 13.9 bhp, 13.9 Nm, shares Hornet chassis/engine de-tuned, LED lamps, digital meter from CBR150R Indonesia; first shown at 2018 Auto Expo India and Dhaka Bike Show 2018. [BikeBD](https://bikebd.com/blog/honda-x-blade-launching-date-in-bangladesh)
- CB Shine SP (125): BD-made version launched July 2019, HET engine 10.7 PS, claimed 65 kmpl; BD production listed from 2019. [Wikipedia Honda Shine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honda_Shine)
- CB Trigger (CB150): introduced 2013, "still available in Bangladeshi market" per Wikipedia (India sales 2013-2015, Hornet 160R successor in India). [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honda_CB_Trigger)
- Livo: BD launch date NOT FOUND; appears in Oct 2019 cashback offer (disc Tk 116,000, drum Tk 107,000). [BHL PR 128 via search](https://bdhonda.com/media-center/pr-details/128)
- Unicorn: BD launch NOT FOUND. BikeBD lists "Not-Available" (Dec 2025 page); used 2010 units on Bikroy; India launch 2004; Dazzler variant 2010 (rear disc). [Wikipedia Unicorn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honda_Unicorn), [BikeBD](https://www.bikebd.com/price/honda-unicorn)
- CD80: first BHL-assembled model 2013 (see first pass). CBR150R etc.: no BD launch dates found.
- Older Honda in BD (BikeBD "oldest 5 models"): CBS125 (~CG125 era, 1976?), CDI H100S (1986), Cub 50 (year inconsistent 1958/2006), CG125 (made in Japan 1976-2008; BD sale years unstated). No importers named.
