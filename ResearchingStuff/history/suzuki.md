# Suzuki in Bangladesh

Access date for all citations: 2026-10-13. Status: PARTIAL.

## Importer / assembler
- Rancon Motor Bikes Ltd (RMBL), Rangs Group unit; exclusive distributor. Suzuki says RMBL began 2012 (infobox says 2013) with a team of three; first shipment arrived summer 2013 and sold out before reaching showrooms. Gazipur plant (140,000 sq ft, 360 staff) established 2018 per Suzuki. [suzuki.com.bd](https://suzuki.com.bd/about)
- 29 Aug 2013: Suzuki and Rancon announced CKD assembly (kits from India), planned Savar plant Tk 80 crore (completion Dec 2013), sales Feb-Mar 2014, 20,000 units year one, three models (110/125/150cc, Tk 1.25-2.5 lakh). Assemblers then paid 100% CKD duty vs 40% for local manufacturers. [Daily Star 2013](https://www.thedailystar.net/news/suzuki-teams-up-with-rancon-to-assemble-motorcycles)
- 22 Aug 2015: Rancon opened Dhaka flagship showroom (Tejgaon-Gulshan Link Rd) displaying Gixxer, Hayate 110, GS150R (six-gear), LETS (auto), Slingshot Plus; prices Tk 139,950-249,950; Gazipur assembly unit (>1.5 lakh sq ft), 50+ dealers. [Daily Star 2015](https://www.thedailystar.net/node/131485)
- Pre-2012 Suzuki (Max100, Samurai, Zeus, etc.) history in BD: NOT FOUND.

[BikeBD](https://bikebd.com/blog/suzuki-launches-gixxer-sf-fi-intruder-abs-fi)

# Second pass (access date 2026-10-13)

- Gixxer 155 (original, carb): India launch early Sept 2014; BD bookings 27-29 Nov 2014 (Bangladesh Shooting Sports Federation), expected Feb-Mar 2015, expected price Tk 240,000; showrooms after 25 Jan 2015 per Rancon; reported price later Tk 235,000. 154.9cc, 14.8 PS / 14 Nm (BD sources say 14.6 BHP), 135 kg, front disc / rear drum, SJCS oil jet cooling, 5-speed, LED tail. BRTA permits 49-155cc (article's statement). Display of GS150R, Hayate, Slingshot at air-gun event, no Gixxer yet. [BikeBD alert](https://bikebd.com/blog/launching-alert-suzuki-gixxer-155), [BikeBD first impression](https://www.bikebd.com/blog/suzuki-gixxer-in-bangladesh-first-impression)
- Gixxer SF (faired) came as carb, rear drum, India launch then BD; BD launch date unconfirmed; later "FI" SF with rear disc, then 2019 gen (1 Jul 2020 BD launch) [first pass].
- Samurai 150 (revived name): launched early August (year unstated, ~2020s), Tk 149,950, 149.5cc OHC carb, 8.2 kW, 11.5 Nm, kick+self start, 5-speed, 133 kg, 12.5 L, front disc/rear drum, 2.75-18 / 90/90-18. Original Samurai = early-2000s 100cc 2-stroke (AX100 name elsewhere). [BikeBD](https://www.bikebd.com/blog/suzuki-launches-suzuki-samurai-in-bangladesh)
- Max 100: India TVS-Suzuki 1998-ish, 98.2cc 2-stroke 7.7 bhp 4-speed (India sources). One low-quality undated BD page says it was popular in 1990s BD then stopped, no importer; UNVERIFIED. No Zeus source found. [Bikes4Sale](https://www.bikes4sale.in/details/suzuki/max/max-100/), [Team-BHP Max 100 thread (India)](https://www.team-bhp.com/forum/motorbikes/242291-my-first-motorcycle-2001-suzuki-max-100-a.html)
