# Premium and grey-import motorcycles in BD

Access date for all citations: 2026-10-13. THIN FILE: only Royal Enfield was verified in first pass.

## Royal Enfield (official)
- Ifad Motors Ltd officially launched Hunter, Classic, Bullet, Meteor on 21 Oct 2024; prices Tk340,000 (Hunter) to Tk435,000 (Meteor); locally assembled at Ifad's facility in Chauddagram, Cumilla. [Bangladesh Monitor](https://bangladeshmonitor.com.bd/en/royal-enfield-launched-in-bangladesh), [Daily Star](https://d11.thedailystar.net/shift/news/royal-enfield-motorcycles-launched-bangladesh-prices-start-tk-34-lakh-3732771)
- Followed a rule change raising the allowed limit to 375 cc for motorcycles previously restricted above 165 cc. [Dhaka Tribune](https://www.dhakatribune.com/bangladesh/362766/royal-enfield-officially-launches-in-bangladesh), [350cc explainer](https://www.dhakatribune.com/bangladesh/325429/350cc-motorcycles-what-you-need-to-know) (details beyond snippet UNVERIFIED)
- Earlier items: "Royal Enfields to hit the streets from May" ([Daily Star](https://online91.thedailystar.net/tech-startup/news/royal-enfields-hit-streets-bangladesh-may-3526211)) and "start sales without pre booking" ([BikeBD](https://bikebd.com/blog/royal-enfield-start-sales-without-pre-booking)) - dates not extracted; fetch to confirm.
