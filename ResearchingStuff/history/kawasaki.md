# Kawasaki in Bangladesh

Access date for all citations: 2026-10-13. Status: PARTIAL.

## Importer and history
- Official operations began early 2018 with Asian Motorbikes Limited (BikeBD claim; no independent confirmation). In the 1980s-90s Kawasaki bikes came only via unofficial individual imports with no parts/after-sales. Showrooms listed: Taiba Motors (Badda), Vulcan Lifestyle (Eskaton), Asian Motorbikes (Tejgaon). [BikeBD Kawasaki](https://www.bikebd.com/brand/kawasaki)
- Kawasaki Ninja 125 and Z125 launched at Hotel Lakeshore, Banani, evening of 31 July (year not stated; a price page labels the Ninja 125 as 2022). Ninja 125 Tk 499,000, Z125 Tk 469,000; dual-channel ABS; Ninja 125 parts from Ninja 250SL, Z125 from Z250SL; sold at flagship showroom and Rampura dealer. BD displacement limit 165cc cited as reason bigger Ninjas stayed out. [BikeBD](https://bikebd.com/blog/kawasaki-ninja-125-launched-bangladesh)

## Models listed by BikeBD
Ninja 125 (2022) 499k; D-Tracker 150 SE 415k; KLX 150BF 395k (cards say 144cc); Z125 PRO (discontinued, last 250k, odd vs launch price; CONFLICT, may be a different/clone listing); discontinued with no data: KDX200, Eliminator 250, Ninja 250R, Ninja 250R 1992. Upcoming (expected only): Ninja 250/300, Z250, KLX 230/250/300R, Versys X300, W175, KX250/250F, J300. [BikeBD Kawasaki](https://www.bikebd.com/brand/kawasaki)
- Owner lore: a BikeBD user write-up "Kawasaki KW-80 11 years riding experience" exists but was not read (listing metadata only).

Not covered: older KW-80/Boxer-type models, grey-market Ninja history, variants, faults.
