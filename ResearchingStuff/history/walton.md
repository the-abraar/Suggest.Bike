# Walton motorcycles - BD model history

Access date for all citations: 2026-10-13. First-pass research; gaps flagged.

## Company
- Walton Motors / Walton Group, HQ Chandra, Kaliakoir, Gazipur. Wikipedia gives conflicting founding: infobox 2008, body "1977 as subsidiary of Walton Group" (CONFLICT). Describes Walton as first motorcycle manufacturer in BD, 80-150 cc range; 2011 articles mention exports to Middle East and Ghana. Page carries a notability warning (July 2025). [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walton_Motors)
- A 2016 motorcycle launch could NOT be confirmed. A consumer site implies motorcycle factory around 2007-2008 (UNVERIFIED).

## SECOND PASS
- FOUNDING CONFLICT PARTLY RESOLVED: Walton Group Wikipedia: R.B. Group founded 1977 by S.M. Nazrul Islam (TV imports), "debuted as Walton Group" 2008 with refrigerators; no motorcycle start date given. [Wikipedia Walton Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walton_Group) BikeBD says Walton Hi-Tech Industries Ltd "established 2006", brand since 1977; Chandra plant makes motorcycle engines (no outside engine supplier named). [BikeBD](https://bikebd.com/blog/walton-bangladesh) Another snippet: formed in present organogram 2002, construction complete 2006, operations 2008 [SlideShare](https://www.slideshare.net/slideshow/walton-bd-ltd-presentation/79692511) (snippet only). The Wikipedia "Walton Motors 2008/1977" lines are therefore the group, not a motorcycle-specific date. Walton wasn't mentioned in The Daily Star's Sept 2013 market piece. [Daily Star](https://www.thedailystar.net/node/1149718)
- Earliest dated evidence of Walton motorcycles: BikeBD blog "Walton Motorcycle Bangladesh And There Products" 15 Jul 2013. Xplore review 4 Jun 2015; "Fusion NX 125 ... launched 2 months back" 11 Jul 2015; Fusion 125EX review 27 Sep 2015. [BikeBD category](https://bikebd.com/blog/category/walton-motorcycle-bangladesh) The motorcycle launch year is thus "by 2013 (blog evidence); official launch date NOT FOUND". Earlier "first manufacturer" claim: Runner prospectus also claims first (runner.md).
- Fusion NX 125 is a distinct name from Fusion 125/125EX (an NX variant existed in 2015 with engine/suspension upgrades per BikeBD); NX vs EX technical differences NOT FOUND.
