# Vintage and legacy motorcycles in BD

Access date for all citations: 2026-10-13. THIN FILE.

## Yamaha RX100 (2-stroke)
BikeBD: production began around 1985 (Japan/India), 98.2 cc 2-stroke, 11.2 BHP; successors RX115, RX135. [BikeBD legends](https://bikebd.com/blog/legendary-discontinued-motorcycles-of-bangladesh) CartTrade (India-focused) says late 1984 launch, ran to 1996 (CONFLICT on year). [CarTrade](https://www.cartrade.com/news/the-cult-status-of-yamaha-rx100-and-rx135-days-in-india-124831.html) BD import years: NOT FOUND. Bikroy lists RX 100. [Bikroy](https://bikroy.com/bn/motorcycles-and-scooters/yamaha-rx-100)

## Other discontinued (BikeBD "legendary" list)
Bajaj Discover 135 (stopped 2010; 134.21 cc DTS-i); Yamaha FZ-S V1 (debut 2008, 153 cc); Hero Honda CBZ Xtreme (2006-2012, 149.2 cc); Honda CB Trigger 150 (stopped 2018 per Honda India; parts still available). Same BikeBD source; reasons for ending are the author's guess.

## Vespa
Sold unofficially since the 1980s, import/distribution halted late 1990s (BikeBD). [BikeBD Vespa](https://testserver.bikebd.com/brand/vespa) About 5,000 Vespas nationwide; Dhaka most, then Jashore, Chattogram, Rajshahi, Mymensingh; used by salesmen and traders. [TBS](https://www.tbsnews.net/node/556590) Dhaka Vespa meet 2022 had 1960s+ models. [Daily Star](https://www.thedailystar.net/tech-startup/news/take-look-inside-the-grand-vespa-meet-dhaka-3212446) Official return via Runner (2019): see chinese-and-other-brands.md.

## Context
Motorcycles popular from 1980s (BikeBD); about 99% on road are locally assembled (Daily Star). [Daily Star](https://www.thedailystar.net/supplements/long-ride/news/how-bangladesh-became-motorcycle-nation-4068976)

## SECOND PASS
- Vespa Club Bangladesh founded 2013 (Facebook group, Rupok Rahaman); ~11,000 members; Oct 2020 meet 150+ Vespas (1961 VBB 150, 1964 VBB, 1974 Super Beetle). Parts are the main problem: classic carburetor up to Tk42,000; full restoration Tk5-10 lakh; cheaper with Indian parts. Vintage import route not explained. [Daily Star](https://www.thedailystar.net/shift/news/scoot-over-old-school-cool-1993921)
- Honda legacy: Honda production/wholesale in BD since 1972 under technical collaboration with Atlas Bangladesh Ltd (BSEC subsidiary); Sept 2012 JV agreement Honda 70% / BSEC 30%. [Honda release](https://global.honda/en/newsroom/news/2012/c120927beng.html) Hero Honda assembled by Atlas, Bajaj by Uttara Group in 2012-13 (Bajaj 49% share, Hero Honda 16%). [Daily Star 2013](https://www.thedailystar.net/node/1149718) Prothom Alo says Uttara began Bajaj around 1985 (Uttara's own profile says 1988: CONFLICT). [Prothom Alo](https://www.prothomalo.com/business/সাত-কোম্পানির-সাতকাহন)
- Bikroy lists old units: Suzuki AX4 1984, Honda CB 1996, LML NV 2-stroke, Yamaha RX100. [AX4](https://bikroy.com/motorcycles-and-scooters/suzuki-ax4-1984), [LML](https://bikroy.com/motorcycles-and-scooters/lml-nv-2-stroke-ks)
