# Chinese, Taiwanese and other non-Japanese/Indian brands in BD

Access date for all citations: 2026-10-13. First pass; BikeBD brand pages are the main source (they are listing sites with internal inconsistencies, flagged). Distributor claims conflict in places.

## Keeway (Hungarian-Chinese Qianjiang group)
- In BD since 2014; SpeedOZ Ltd official distributor until 2018; Aftab Automobile Ltd (Navana Group) from Feb 2019; assembly at Fouzdarhat, Chittagong. [BikeBD Keeway](https://www.bikebd.com/brand/keeway)
- CONFLICT: another BikeBD profile says DHS Motorbikes Ltd (Rangs Group) took official distribution of Keeway and Benelli. [BikeBD DHS](https://bikebd.com/bikeimporter/dhs-motorbikes-limited) vs [Aftab](https://bikebd.com/bikeimporter/aftab-automobile-limited). Dates unclear; verify.
- Models (list prices BDT): RKR 165 215,000; TXM 150 179,000; Superlight 150 177,000; K-Light 167,500; RKS 150 CBS 149,900; RKS 150 Sports 149,900; RKV 150 (2016) 139,900; K Blade 125 scooter 129,000; RKS 125 118,500; RK 125 109,500; RKS 100 99,900; Magnet 100 86,900. Discontinued (no price): SR125, Fact Evo 125, Flash, Matrix, Patagonian Eagle 250, RK 200, RK V125C, V-Cruise 302C/V302C, FACT X 125, V Cruise 125, K300 SF/N/R, SR 250, MBP C1002V, MBP M502N, Benda Darkflag, Benda LFC 700, Benda LFS 700, K-Light 250V, Vieste 300, Sixties 300i. Upcoming: Cafe Racer 152, RKF 125, SIP 125, Hypevolt-R. Several models appear in both active and discontinued lists (page error). Same source.

## SECOND PASS

### Distributor conflicts
- Keeway/Benelli: BikeBD article on Aftab (announcing a 23 Feb 2019 Cox's Bazar launch) says Aftab Automobiles (Navana Group; previously Mahindra distributor) is the new official distributor replacing Speedoz; it does not mention DHS. [BikeBD](https://www.bikebd.com/blog/aftab-automobiles-ltd-keeway-benelli) The DHS (Rangs) page is undated and its claim rests on that single page. Verdict: Aftab 2019 is best supported (multiple pages); DHS UNVERIFIED, may be later change or error. No primary (company/news) confirmation found. Note BikeBD "last updated" stamps are rolling site dates, not publication dates.
- Zontes: BikeBD launch article (undated by a rolling stamp; snippet cites 14 Jan 2025): Mototech Industry is official distributor, MotoSolution BD Ltd markets/sells (showroom Matikata, Dhaka). Not conflicting: distributor = Mototech, retailer/marketer = MotoSolution. Launch models ZT155 U 349,000 / U1 379,000 / G1 389,000 / Z2 349,000; engine 155 cc single, 4-valve, 18.5 BHP, EFI, 6-speed, slipper clutch, Bosch dual-channel ABS, Euro V. A 2021 Victory Day offer article says Zontes launched "this year 2021", so an earlier launch than 2025 is possible (CONFLICT, unresolved). [BikeBD](https://bikebd.com/blog/zontes-motorcycles-launches-in-bangladesh), [2021 offer](https://www.bikebd.com/blog/zontes-victory-day-offer-2021)

### Taro
Taro Bangla Ltd (Sikder Group, Banani). Rebranded Chinese-built sportbikes; some engines Lifan-sourced (cited example Valentino 400 series); GP 1 Special Edition with ABS launched at Dhaka Bike Show 2019. Active: GP 1 V4 250 (399,000), Xsuv Fi ABS 150 (360,000), GP 1 V3 (356,000), Venom 160 (350,000), Vampire 150 Fi ABS (335,000), GP 1 Special Edition (321,000), Imola 150 scooter (185,000). Discontinued: GP 1 Sport, GP 2, F16 CT Max 125. Upcoming: GP 2 200, GP-1 Naked Sport, GP1 250R. FI vs carb: models named "Fi" are EFI; others not stated. [BikeBD Taro](https://bikebd.com/brand/taro)

### Roadmaster
Roadmaster Motors Ltd (Update Group; also distributes Dayun). Assembled at own plant in BD from imported Chinese parts under technical collaboration with Guangzhou Dayang Motorcycle Co. Current: Delight 98.5 cc (84,900), Prime 100 (76,900), Rex 79 cc (70,900), Prime 80 (60,900). Discontinued: Rapido 165, Rapido 150, Velocity 125, Velocity 100. Upcoming Predator 149, Cupid 107. [BikeBD Roadmaster](https://bikebd.com/brand/roadmaster) Roadmaster mopeds attended 2020 Vespa meet (Daily Star, below).

### Hyosung
No confirmed official import. BikeBD (rolling stamp Oct 2026): importer unnamed "due to business secrecy"; GT125R (full fairing) and GT125 (naked): 124 cc air/oil-cooled DOHC 8-valve 75-degree V-twin, 14.65 BHP, CARBURETOR, 5-speed; no price announced. Hyosung founded 1978, initially Suzuki-design bikes under licence; sold to S&T 2007, Kolao 2014 (KR Motors). GT250R/N listed "upcoming" at est. 510,990 BDT (aggregator estimate). [BikeBD](https://bikebd.com/blog/hyosung-gt125r-gt125-upcoming-motorcycle-in-bd), [GT250R](https://bikebd.com/price/hyosung-gt250r) Used Hyosung 125/250 ads exist on Bikroy (grey). [Bikroy](https://bikroy.com/bn/motorcycles-and-scooters/hyosung-125-250)
