# Bajaj in Bangladesh

Citation convention: `[Sn]` = source n in the Sources list at the bottom; all accessed 2026-10-13. Status flags: **VERIFIED** (stated by a cited page), **CONFLICT**, **UNVERIFIED** (listing-site inference or not found). Caveat: BikeBD pages carry unreliable "last updated" stamps (many show 12/13-Oct-2026 regardless of real article date) and an own disclaimer that data may be inaccurate [S1]. Research tool coverage was limited; see "Gaps" at the end. Nothing here is invented; absent facts are listed as gaps.

## Importer / distributor
- Uttara Motors Ltd is the sole Bajaj distributor; part of Uttara Group (founded 1972, per BikeBD); distributed Bajaj motorcycles/three-wheelers since the 1980s; earlier imported Vespa 150 from India; later introduced "Kawasaki Bajaj" four-strokes [S1]. Sole-distributor claim also in TBS News Dhaka Motor Show '22 coverage [S9]. Warranty 2 years / 30,000 km [S1].
- Dhaka Times (Bangla, 2020) names "Uttara Motors Limited" as Bajaj's authorised dealer [S4].

## Pulsar 150 (the BD volume model)
- Global history (Wikipedia [S2]): 2001 first gen 150/180, single spark + carburettor; 2003 DTSi (Digital Twin Spark Ignition) +1 hp; 2005 17" alloys, 13.5 hp; 2006 UG III (digital readout, LED tail-light); 2009 UG IV (150 = 14.09 hp); 2010 UG 4.5 (150 = 15.06 hp). Wikipedia does not name UG I, II or V. (Single- vs twin-spark is therefore a 2001 vs 2003+ difference globally; whether any 2001-2003 single-spark Pulsar 150 was sold in BD is **UNVERIFIED**. Bikroy has a "Pulsar 150 2003" search page [S12], but a listing year is not proof of model.)
- Bangladesh 2020: 150cc variants sold were Neon, Single Disc, Double/Twin Disc, all DTS-i; 135cc Pulsar no longer sold; NS160 also sold [S4][S5]. Dhaka Times says Single Disc "BS4" (later text "BS4.5, engine guard, new graphics"), Twin Disc "BS5" (sic; likely a journalist's term) [S4][S5] — **CONFLICT/unclear emission label**.
- Twin Disc (2020 refresh, per S5): no kicker, no chain cover, blue-toned meter, changed clip-on bars, thicker forks, split seat, styled grab rail. Single Disc: engine guard, new graphics. Neon: colour-scheme variant. S5 says the Indian-market Neon/TD had ABS/BS6/FI (India differs from BD).
- Pulsar 150 Neon BD launch: price BDT 154,900; 150cc air-cooled DTS-i, 13.8 BHP @8000, 13.4 Nm @6000, 5-speed, **carburettor**, 15 L tank, 144 kg, front 240 mm disc, rear 130 mm drum; cosmetic differences only (colours, alloy grab rail, 3D logo, coloured wheels) [S6]. Launch date not stated.
- Pulsar 150 Twin Disc ABS BD launch: BDT 196,900; **single-channel ABS on front only**; 260 mm front disc, 230 mm rear disc, 1345 mm wheelbase, Nitrox twin rear shocks, 15 L, 144 kg; only change vs Twin Disc is the ABS. Range prices in that article: TD ABS 196,900; TD 180,900; Single Disc 169,900. "Third Bajaj with single-channel ABS after NS160 ABS and Avenger Street ABS" [S7]. Date not stated.
- Spec conflicts: Bajaj BD pages list 14 PS @8000 / 13.4 Nm; Bajaj BD blog elsewhere gives 12 PS / 11 Nm [S3]; BikeBD says 13.8 BHP [S6][S7]. Single-disc front disc 240 mm / rear drum 130 mm [S3].
- Price over time (BDT, all listing/press, dates unreliable): original Pulsar 150 1,86,900 then -4,000 cut "10-31 Oct 2019" [S10]; Twin Disc 1,92,750 (undated, Bajaj BD) [S3]; Dec 2025 Winter Fest: TD ABS 227,000 (was 235,100) [S11]; list Sept 2026: TD ABS 231,100; TD 217,000; Single Disc ABS 210,000; Pulsar 150 205,200; "Pulsar 150 2018" 172,500 (discontinued); Neon 154,900 (discontinued) [S1].
- Bajaj BD page says Pulsar 150 "upgraded again to the UG5 variant" without saying what changed [S3 search snippet via bikebd]; **UG4 vs UG5 differences in BD: not found**.
- Fuel system: Neon explicitly carburettor [S6]; no BD Pulsar 150 FI variant found in sources (Indian TD "FI" mentioned only for India [S5]).

## New dated BD facts
- **Pulsar AS150**: official launch at Bangabandhu Intl Conference Centre; BD launch ~7 months after India's May 2015 debut (late 2015/early 2016; the article refers to "within February of 2016"); 4-valve DTS-i, two plugs, 16.8 BHP/13 Nm, 12 L, 240 mm disc/130 mm drum, 143 kg [S22]. Whether still sold: not stated.
- **Discover 150F**: launched at New Eskaton centre; 144.8cc 4-valve DTS-i ExhausTEC, 14.5 PS@8500, 12.75 Nm, **carburettor BS 26**, 5-speed, 130 kg drum, 10 L tank, price 186,500 (page conflicts internally on rear tyre/top speed/weight); launch year not stated [S23].
- **Pulsar 150 "2017" BS4 version**: quietly rolled out "since 27 March" (year not printed; article title says 2017), BDT 169,000, price unchanged though India's rose. Versus prior version: **13.8 BHP @8000 (down 1 BHP), 13.4 Nm (up 1 Nm)**; smaller bore, longer stroke, changed bottom end/crankcase; **5-map CDI vs 2-map**; bigger muffler; tubeless tyres; laser-edge graphics, gunmetal engine, new cluster; 240 mm front disc; AHO required; colours Nuclear Blue, Laser Black, Dyno Red [S24]. This is the best BD evidence of the "UG4.5 to BS4" generation break. It also fits the BD spec change in S6/S7 (13.8 BHP).
- **Pulsar N250**: BD launch 27 Nov 2023, 250cc oil-cooled 24.5 PS/21.5 Nm, dual-channel ABS, slipper clutch (New Age) [S25]. **Pulsar N250 FI DC ABS and N160 FI DC ABS (16 PS/14.65 Nm)** plus "Pulsar Studio" launched 10 Jan 2026 (TBS) [S26]. 
- **Uttara factory**: Savar, near Dhaka, ~300,000 units/yr, 15 branches, 300+ dealers, "marketed Bajaj for 4+ decades" [S26].
- Pulsar 150 TD ABS launch price 196,900 and Twin Disc 180,900, Single Disc 169,900 [S7] (undated).

## Generations / single vs twin spark in BD (what the evidence supports)
- Global: 2001 single-spark carb Pulsar (round "classic" headlamp); 2003 DTSi twin-spark [S2]. XBHP: "classic (round headlamp) 150 has a single plug engine"; UG3 uses UCAL/Mikuni BS26 carb; UG4.5 vs UG3 little performance difference; a 2011 UG4.5 had a kick lever [S27]. - BD owner review (BikeBD, UG4.5): 240 mm front disc/130 mm rear drum, MRF Zapper 2.75-17 / 100/90 tyres, MF battery, 42-45 km/l city, 48-50 km/l highway under 55 km/h; no mention of years [S29].
- UG1-UG5 to BD year mapping and any BD-spec differences: **still not found**. Twin-spark claim for current BD bikes is Bajaj's own ("Twin-Spark DTSi") [S20].

## Owner-community faults (Indian forums; BD relevance unconfirmed)
- XBHP Pulsar 150 thread [S27]: overheating from lean mixture (retune, check airbox hose); idle ticking at ~1,800-2,000 rpm from loose timing chain / failed cam chain tensioner; loss of mid-range after 5-5.5k rpm cured by a different carb; hard shifting when oil thin; oil leak. Chain slack 25-30 mm; Bosch UR3DC plugs mentioned.
- Search summaries of BikeWale/XBHP: BS6 Pulsar 150 overheating and throttle-body replacement within 5,000 km; Pulsar 180 hard shifting at 150 km (clutch switch/loose clutch); battery dying [S30]. Not re-fetched in full. BD owner (UG4.5): clutch heavy, gear shifting less smooth than Hero/Honda, loud at high rpm, water damages electricals, cheap footrests, fake parts common [S29].
- **Recalls**: none found for BD (searched; only an unrelated EV page).
