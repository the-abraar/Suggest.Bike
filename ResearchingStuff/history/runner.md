# Runner (Runner Automobiles PLC) - BD model history

Access date for all citations: 2026-10-13. COVERAGE NOTE: first-pass research (about 20 fetches). Items marked UNVERIFIED or NOT FOUND need a second pass. Nothing here is invented; gaps are listed explicitly.

## Company summary
- Founded by Hafizur Rahman Khan (chairman). Wikipedia infobox says 4 June 2000, history text says incorporated July 2000 (CONFLICT). Became public limited in Jan 2012; started as importer/trader, then manufacturer. Plant at Bhaluka, Mymensingh, ~500 bikes/day capacity. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Runner_Automobiles)
- Past technical collaborators: Dayang Motors and Lohia Machinery "Freedom" (Wikipedia). UM Motorcycles agreement 2016, "UM-Runner" bikes reached market Q2 2018. Honda/Yamaha are NOT mentioned on that page, so the "Runner uses Honda/Yamaha-clone engines" claim is UNVERIFIED. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Runner_Automobiles)
- Also official distributor of Vespa and Aprilia (Piaggio) in BD via Runner Automobile Bangladesh Ltd; Vespa official activity began with Dhaka Motor Show 2019. [BikeBD Vespa](https://www.bikebd.com/brand/vespa)
- Sole agent of Yadea electric scooters in BD. [Financial Express](https://thefinancialexpress.com.bd/stock/bangladesh/runner-to-take-eco-friendly-two-wheeler-market-share-with-chinese-scooters)

## SECOND PASS (primary source: IPO prospectus)
Primary source: Runner Automobiles Ltd IPO prospectus (issue date 6 Jan 2019), hosted at [BRAC EPL PDF](https://www.bracepl.com/brokerage/assets/research/comsector/Runner_Automobile_Prospectus.pdf) (first 8 MB downloaded and text-extracted, accessed 2026-10-13). Also [Financial Express IPO piece](https://today.thefinancialexpress.com.bd/print/runner-auto-to-float-ipo-worth-tk-10b) (snippet only).

- FOUNDING CONFLICT RESOLVED: began as proprietorship "M/S Runner"; incorporated as Runner Automobiles Ltd as a private company on **4 July 2000** (incorporation no. C-40658(17)/2000); converted to public limited on **26 January 2012**. (Wikipedia's 4 June 2000 infobox date is therefore wrong; July 2000 matches.)
- Prospectus says "since incorporation in 2000 ... from being an assembler to becoming the first motorcycle manufacturer in Bangladesh" (company's own claim; Walton also claims "first" - see walton.md).
- As of 30 Jun 2017: 691 employees, capacity 100,000 two-wheelers/yr (note 39), range 50-150 cc, about 13 models. Factory at Paragaon, Barachala, Bhaluka, Mymensingh; registered office 138/1 Tejgaon I/A. (Wikipedia's 500/day figure is unverified against this.)
- Brands named: "Dayang Runner, Freedom Runner, LML Freedom and Runner". Motorcycle list: AD 80S, AD 80S Deluxe, Cheeta, F100-6A, DY 50, Bullet, Royal+, Knight Rider, Turbo, Turbo 125, Freedom, Kite, Kite+.
- Luyang Northern EK Chore MC Co. Ltd (Dayang) is named as a major raw-material supplier (single-supplier risk factor). So Dayang = confirmed technical/parts source. No Honda/Yamaha engine licence is mentioned: the "Honda/Yamaha-clone engine" claim stays UNVERIFIED (clone status is an outside assertion).
- Subsidiary Freedom Motors Ltd (reg. 2 Feb 2008; 99.99% held) assembles Freedom brand; "Lohia/LML Freedom" link: brand name "LML Freedom" appears, confirming an LML tie; details not extracted.
- UM International LLC (USA) agreement signed 28 Sept 2016: UM-Runner Xtreet and Renegade (100-150 cc planned). Bajaj RE three-wheeler distributor (2,000+ units sold 2017). Exports to Nepal.
- Competitors named by Runner: Uttara Motors, TVS Auto BD, Niloy Motors, Rancon Motor Bikes Ltd, Navana 2 Wheelers.
